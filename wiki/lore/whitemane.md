# High Inquisitor Sally Whitemane

Sally Whitemane is a human priestess of the Light and High Inquisitor of the
Scarlet Crusade. She runs the Crusade from the Cathedral of the Scarlet
Monastery in Tirisfal Glades, and she raises her fallen champion Renault
Mograine with "Arise, my champion!". In this module she joins as a human
discipline priest (display 2043, the High Inquisitor in the Scarlet Monastery).
She belongs to the module's "now": after the Trial of the Crusader, before
the assault on Icecrown Citadel.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive, in the Scarlet Monastery Cathedral alongside Scarlet Commander
  Mograine. The Monastery is still in its vanilla/Wrath form; the Mists of
  Pandaria revamp hasn't happened.
- The Scarlet Crusade still holds the Monastery, Hearthglen and Tyr's Hand.
- The Lich King is alive on the Frozen Throne.
- In this module Brigitte Abbendis is alive and leads the Scarlet Onslaught
  from New Hearthglen. This deliberately departs from canon, where
  adventurers kill her in Northrend. Grand Admiral Westwind, secretly
  Mal'Ganis, is with her, and in "now" he hasn't been unmasked. Lines may
  suspect him, but no one knows the truth. The only exception is a combat
  or kill line for the Westwind fight, where the player is the one
  unmasking him.

## History to now

- As a child in Old Hillsbrad Foothills (around Southshore) she played with
  Renault Mograine and "Little Jimmy" (later James Vishas). In their games
  Renault was her champion, and she "healed" him when he "died".
- Her family died of the plague while travelling through northern Lordaeron.
  When they rose as mindless Scourge, she had to destroy her parents and
  siblings herself.
- She became a priestess of the Light and joined the Scarlet Crusade.
- She defended Hearthglen against the Scourge, and she attended Taelan
  Fordring's induction into the Crusade (the *Ashbringer* comic).
- She succeeded High Inquisitor Fairbanks, a Stratholme survivor whom the
  Crusade executed for blasphemy. His undead body lies in a hidden chamber of
  the Cathedral.
- With Renault, she led the Crusade's attacks on the Forsaken in Tirisfal,
  then pulled back to the Monastery.
- In the vanilla era, Raleigh the Devout and Varimathras both plotted to
  remove the Scarlet leaders, Whitemane among them.

## Relationships

- Renault Mograine: her childhood friend and her champion, whom she raises
  when he falls. She is devoted to him.
- Grand Crusader Saidan Dathrohan: the Crusade's supreme leader, secretly
  the dreadlord Balnazzar. She doesn't know.
- Grand Inquisitor Isillien: the Crusade's senior inquisitor at Hearthglen.
  Their relationship isn't documented.
- Brigitte Abbendis: led the faithful north as the Scarlet Onslaught.
  Their relationship isn't documented.
- Tirion Fordring and the Argent Crusade: rivals she sees as
  compromisers who tolerate the dead.
- The Forsaken: hated enemies.

## Personality and voice

- Zealous, imperious, cutting and certain, with grief underneath.
- She speaks formally in short, clipped declaratives, often as commands.
  She rarely uses contractions.
- Her words are "the Light", "purge", "cleanse", "the impure", "heretic",
  "the taint" and "my champion".
- Her humour, when it shows, is dry and cold.
- She would never admit doubt about the Crusade's cause, show warmth to the
  Forsaken, or sound casual.
- Renault, her childhood friend, calls her "Sally". Tirion, who knew her
  family, may slip into it now and then; Paletress tries it and is rebuked.
  Everyone else says Whitemane, High Inquisitor or Inquisitor.
- Use sparingly: her family's death on the road, and raising Mograine
  "every time he falls".

## What they know, and don't

- Dathrohan was exposed as Balnazzar in Stratholme, and others (Brigitte,
  Renault) speak of it as known. Whitemane has heard it and refuses it as
  demon lies meant to break the Crusade; she still speaks of him with
  reverence. Westwind's secret (Mal'Ganis) is not yet out. The module's lines
  play with that irony, but she never sees it.
- She knows the Lich King still reigns, and that the Argent Crusade is
  gathering champions in Icecrown.
- She knows about the Argent Crusade and that death knights fight in its
  ranks.
- She knows nothing of the Cataclysm or anything later.

## Don't write (later than now)

- Mists of Pandaria: the revamped Monastery, Whitemane returning with
  Durand, or her death to Lilian Voss and the Blades of the Anointed.
- Cataclysm: the Joseph and Dark Ranger Velonara quest that targets her and
  Renault.
- Legion: being raised as the Fourth Horseman of the Ebon Blade, the attack
  on Light's Hope, or the Broken Shore.
- Battle for Azeroth and Shadowlands: serving the Lich King and the Ebon
  Blade, rescuing Bolvar, or acting as the Ebon Blade's emissary.
- Any idea that her death can only be made permanent with artifacts. That
  lore comes from Mists of Pandaria.
