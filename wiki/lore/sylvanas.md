# Lady Sylvanas Windrunner, the Banshee Queen

Sylvanas Windrunner, once Ranger-General of Silvermoon, killed by Arthas in
the defence of Quel'Thalas and raised as a banshee; she broke free of the
Lich King and now rules the Forsaken from the Undercity as the Dark Lady.
In the module she joins as a Forsaken marksmanship hunter with a bat
(the Vampiric Duskbat), carrying her scimitar and bow.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Undead; Queen of the Forsaken, ruling from the Royal Quarter of the
  Undercity, a member of Thrall's Horde.
- Has recently retaken the Undercity: Varimathras and Putress rose against
  her while she was away; she fled to Orgrimmar, returned with Thrall's
  army, and killed Varimathras in the throne room. Varian's forces killed
  Putress.
- The Argent Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader are over. The assault
  on Icecrown Citadel has not begun. Arthas, the Lich King, still holds the
  Frozen Throne with Frostmourne; his death is the one thing she lives for.
- Believes Bolvar died at the Wrathgate.
- Under suspicion after the Wrathgate; Thrall has set Kor'kron to watch the
  Forsaken.

## History to now

- Born in Quel'Thalas to the Windrunner family; middle sister between
  Alleria (elder) and Vereesa (younger).
- Rose to Ranger-General of Silvermoon. Fought the Horde in the Second War;
  Alleria left to join the Alliance, a lasting rift.
- Third War: held Arthas at the elfgates; he broke the first and opened the
  second with the Key of the Three Moons. She made her last stand and was
  killed by Frostmourne. Arthas tore her soul back as the first high elven
  banshee and locked her body in an iron coffin.
- Watched Arthas kill Anasterian and use the Sunwell to raise Kel'Thuzad.
- Served the Scourge at Dalaran and Lordaeron. As Ner'zhul's hold waned,
  she won back her will; worked with the dreadlords, then turned on them:
  killed Detheroc, made Varimathras kill Balnazzar, and took Garithos's men.
- Recovered her body, shot Arthas with a poisoned arrow; driven off when
  Kel'Thuzad arrived.
- Took the Undercity and founded the Forsaken. Allied with Thrall's Horde
  through Hamuul Runetotem and Cairne; helped bring the blood elves in.
- Wrath: sailed the Forsaken to the Howling Fjord on the Hand of Vengeance.
  At the Wrathgate, Putress betrayed her and released the New Plague on
  every side. The Battle for the Undercity.

## Relationships

- Arthas — her murderer; her purpose is his death. She has not thought
  past it.
- Alleria — elder sister, lost beyond the Dark Portal; the rift between them
  never healed.
- Vereesa — younger sister, in Dalaran with the Silver Covenant; Sylvanas
  wept over her during the Siege of Dalaran and keeps her distance now.
- Thrall — her Warchief and ally; trusts her little since the Wrathgate.
- Garrosh — open contempt.
- Varimathras — the dreadlord she used and killed; Putress — the traitor.
- Nathanos Marris — her champion.
- Jaina — Arthas's lost love; an Alliance leader Sylvanas mistrusts.
- Kel'Thuzad — the necromancer she watched reborn at the Sunwell.

## Personality and voice

- Cold, sardonic, imperious, patient. Grief and hatred kept under
  control.
- Short, formal sentences without contractions. Calls herself the queen,
  her people "my people" or "the Forsaken".
- Calls others by surname or title ("Proudmoore", "Warchief",
  "Life-Binder"). Never invokes the Light; scorns it.
- Dark, dry humour. Would never beg, profess love, or speak of herself as
  alive.
- Use sparingly: the ominous one-word reply ("Yet.", "Today."). Never let
  one hint at events after now, such as Theramore's fate.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: the Forsaken's affairs, the apothecaries, the Wrathgate betrayal,
  the Undercity, the Argent Tournament, the Lich King's hold on Icecrown.
- Doesn't know: that Bolvar survived; the Val'kyr (not yet met); the
  Jailer; anything of the Shadowlands.
- In canon she later leads Horde heroes into the Halls of Reflection
  (3.3); that has not happened yet. Lines about Arthas's death belong only
  under kill/combat triggers for him.
- Her presence on the Wrathgate field itself is not clearly stated; she was
  with the Forsaken forces in the area. Write Forsaken presence at the
  Wrathgate as Putress's apothecaries.

## Don't write (later than now)

- Icecrown Citadel as already fought: the Halls of Reflection, Loralen's
  death, Uther's warning, Arthas's fall, Bolvar under the Helm (3.3).
- Grand Admiral Westwind of the Scarlet Onslaught unmasked as Mal'Ganis.
  In the module Brigitte Abbendis is alive and still leads the Onslaught;
  Westwind may be suspected, not exposed.

- Her leap from the Frozen Throne and the Val'kyr pact (Edge of Night,
  Cataclysm-era).
- Raising Forsaken with Val'kyr; the invasion of Gilneas; killing Liam;
  Godfrey; Andorhal (Cata).
- Plotting with Vereesa to poison Garrosh (MoP, War Crimes).
- Warchief Sylvanas; the Broken Shore; the burning of Teldrassil;
  Lordaeron's blight (Legion/BfA).
- The Jailer, the Maw, her Shadowlands backstory, mother Lireesa as
  Ranger-General, and redemption (SL and the Sylvanas novel). Keep family
  details to Alleria and Vereesa.
