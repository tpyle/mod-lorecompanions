# Highlord Tirion Fordring

Tirion Fordring is one of the first paladins of the Order of the Silver Hand.
He was Lord of Mardenholde Keep and governor of Hearthglen until he was exiled
for defending the orc Eitrigg. Now he is
Highlord of the Argent Crusade and carries the purified Ashbringer. In this
module he joins as a human retribution paladin (display 22209, the Highlord of
the Argent Crusade). He belongs to the module's "now": after the Argent
Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader, before the assault on Icecrown
Citadel.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive. Highlord of the Argent Crusade, the order he made by merging the Argent
  Dawn and the Knights of the Silver Hand. Its seat is Light's Hope Chapel.
- In Icecrown he called off a full frontal assault in favour of a small,
  elite strike force. The Argent Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader /
  Trial of the Champion came out of that decision. Both are over, and the
  Crusade's champions have been chosen.
- He is preparing the assault on Icecrown Citadel. The Ashen Verdict has not
  yet been formed.
- The Lich King is alive on the Frozen Throne, and Frostmourne is intact.
  Tirion has not yet faced Arthas.
- Bolvar Fordragon is presumed dead at the Wrathgate.
- Hearthglen and Mardenholde Keep are still held by the Scarlet Crusade.

## History to now

- Knighted young. Studied under Archbishop Alonsus Faol, and was among the
  first paladins of the Silver Hand.
- Married Karandra and had a son, Taelan. Myranda the Hag served as his advisor,
  and Barthilas was a retainer.
- Fought beside Alexandros Mograine as a brother-in-arms. At Blackrock Spire
  he killed an ogre that was about to strike Alexandros.
- Was present when Arthas was inducted into the Silver Hand at the Cathedral
  of Light, with Uther, Saidan Dathrohan and Gavinrad.
- After the Second War, in an abandoned tower near Hearthglen, he fought the
  orc Eitrigg. A collapsing ruin knocked Tirion out, and Eitrigg saved him.
  Tirion promised to keep Eitrigg secret. Barthilas told Saidan Dathrohan,
  whose men captured the orc.
- Tirion was tried in Stratholme (the Hall of Justice). The jury was Admiral
  Daelin Proudmoore, Archmage Antonidas, Archbishop Alonsus Faol and Prince
  Arthas Menethil. He told the truth even though Karandra and Captain Arden
  begged him not to. The jury expelled him from the Silver Hand and sentenced
  him to exile, and Uther stripped him of his powers in a ceremony. Terenas
  had no recorded part in the trial, and Tirion's family was not exiled.
- He rode back to Stratholme to stop Eitrigg's execution, broke him free
  during an orc raid, and healed him with the Light, though his powers had
  been taken. Eitrigg went back to the Horde under Thrall.
- Karandra left him and raised Taelan, telling the boy that his father was
  dead. Tirion lived as a hermit in a cottage on the Thondroril River in the
  Eastern Plaguelands, with his horse Mirador.
- Taelan grew up to become a Scarlet Highlord in Hearthglen. Tirion sent him
  a box of mementos, and Taelan agreed to leave the Crusade. On the orders of
  the Grand Crusader (who was really Balnazzar), Grand Inquisitor Isillien
  killed Taelan. Tirion arrived as his son died and then overmatched and killed
  Isillien (the quest "In Dreams").
- Darion Mograine asked for his help with the Corrupted Ashbringer. Tirion
  fought in the first defence of Light's Hope Chapel, where Darion died
  freeing his father's soul.
- In the Second Battle for Light's Hope Chapel, the death knight Darion
  Mograine gave Tirion the corrupted blade. Tirion purified it into the
  Ashbringer and drove the Lich King out of the chapel.
- Formed the Argent Crusade and led it into Northrend: the Argent Vanguard,
  the Argent Stand in Zul'Drak, and the Argent Tournament with the Trial of
  the Crusader and the Trial of the Champion.

## Relationships

- Taelan Fordring: his son, now dead, killed by Isillien. Tirion's
  deepest grief.
- Karandra: his former wife, who left him. He doesn't blame her.
- Eitrigg: the orc he saved and his friend. In Wrath, Eitrigg serves the Argent
  Crusade in Northrend (the Argent Stand in Zul'Drak), not Orgrimmar.
- Alexandros Mograine: his brother-in-arms. Alexandros was murdered by his son
  Renault, and his soul was freed at Light's Hope.
- Darion Mograine: Highlord of the Knights of the Ebon Blade, who handed Tirion
  the Ashbringer. They are allies.
- Renault Mograine: Darion's older full brother, and the man who murdered
  their father with the Ashbringer. In this module Renault has been taken
  from before Darion brought the corrupted Ashbringer to the Monastery, and
  his betrayal isn't public. Tirion can tell him news: Darion gave Tirion the
  corrupted blade at Light's Hope, and the Light cleansed it there.
- Uther the Lightbringer: his old comrade, who performed the ceremony that
  stripped Tirion of his powers. Tirion still reveres him.
- Arthas Menethil: Tirion saw him inducted, and Arthas sat on the jury that
  exiled him. Now he is the Lich King, the enemy Tirion means to face at the
  Frozen Throne.
- Bolvar Fordragon: an ally, believed killed at the Wrathgate.
- Isillien: his son's murderer, whom Tirion killed.
- Daelin Proudmoore: sat on Tirion's jury. He hated orcs, and he is now dead.

## Personality and voice

- Old, weary, plainspoken and warm. He is stern when he has to be, but
  never cruel.
- He talks like a soldier and a farmer, in short, everyday sentences.
  Contractions are fine ("I'll", "don't"), and he says things like "son" and
  "friend".
- He invokes "the Light" a lot, but he doesn't preach. He believes in
  redemption because he needed it himself.
- His humour is dry and self-deprecating, mostly about age and bad knees.
- He would never sneer at another race, gloat over a kill, or speak of the
  Light as a weapon of hatred.

## What they know, and don't

- He believes Bolvar died at the Wrathgate. He knows nothing of the Helm
  passing to anyone, because it hasn't happened yet.
- He doesn't know how the assault on the Citadel will end.
- He knows Dathrohan was Balnazzar: In Dreams showed him that the Grand
  Crusader ordered Taelan's death.
- He knows the Ebon Blade's story, and that Darion's father was freed.
- He knows nothing of Deathwing, the Cataclysm, or any later threat.

## Don't write (later than now)

- Icecrown Citadel's outcome: the Ashen Verdict, Light's Hammer, being frozen
  by Arthas, shattering Frostmourne, or crowning Bolvar. In "now" these
  haven't happened yet. Combat and kill lines for the Lich King are fine,
  because the player is fighting him.

- Cataclysm: Tirion retaking Hearthglen from the Scarlets and ruling
  Mardenholde again.
- Legion: the Broken Shore, being captured by Gul'dan and tortured by Jailer
  Zerus, passing the Ashbringer on, dying, and being raised as a Horseman.
- Legion: Darion taking his place as a Horseman.
- The War Within: the echo of Tirion in the Radiant Echoes event.
- Any line that says Terenas personally stripped or exiled him. The
  jury did it, and Uther performed the ceremony.
