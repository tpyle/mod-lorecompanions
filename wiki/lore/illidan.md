# Illidan Stormrage, the Betrayer

Illidan Stormrage, twin brother of Malfurion, once a sorcerer among the
Highborne, given demon sight by Sargeras, imprisoned for ten thousand years,
self-proclaimed Lord of Outland. In the module he joins as a night elf combat
rogue fighting with the Warglaives of Azzinoth, drawn with his Black Temple
model. He is taken out of time by the bronze dragonflight from before the
Black Temple fell, and has been told he dies there.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- In the world's "now" (after the Trial of the Crusader, before the assault
  on Icecrown Citadel; the Lich King still holds the Frozen Throne), Illidan
  is dead: slain in the Black Temple by
  adventurers with Akama and Maiev Shadowsong. Maiev's wardens took his body.
- The companion is the living Illidan, taken from the Black Temple before
  that assault. He knows he dies there, at Maiev's hand, betrayed by Akama.
- He was not told everything since. He knows, as prophecy, that Arthas
  falls at Icecrown, but in this "now" it has not happened yet. Kael'thas's
  betrayal and fall are told news.

## History to the Black Temple

- Born with Malfurion in ancient Kalimdor; studied as a sorcerer. Loved
  Tyrande, who chose Malfurion.
- In the War of the Ancients, Sargeras burned out his eyes, replaced them
  with orbs of mystic fire and marked him with fel tattoos.
- After the Sundering, filled vials from the Well of Eternity and poured them
  into the lake atop Mount Hyjal, making a new Well. Malfurion had him
  imprisoned beneath Hyjal, guarded by Maiev Shadowsong, for ten thousand
  years.
- In the Third War, Tyrande freed him over Malfurion's protest. He consumed
  the Skull of Gul'dan in Felwood (then part of Ashenvale), became demonic,
  killed Tichondrius, and was banished. He left before the Battle of Mount
  Hyjal; he was not there when Archimonde died.
- Fought Arthas in Felwood to an impasse. Struck a bargain with Kil'jaeden to
  destroy the Frozen Throne; used the Eye of Sargeras at Dalaran to try to
  break Northrend's ice, and was stopped by Malfurion and Maiev.
- Saved Tyrande; was let go on the promise never to trouble the night elves
  again. Recaptured by Maiev, rescued by Lady Vashj and Kael'thas.
- In Outland, shut Magtheridon's gates, took the Black Temple with naga,
  blood elves and Akama's Broken, and chained Magtheridon beneath Hellfire
  Citadel. Declared himself Lord of Outland.
- Marched on Icecrown and lost to Arthas at the foot of the glacier; fled
  back to Outland to hide from Kil'jaeden.

## Relationships

- Malfurion — twin; rivalry and resentment; his jailer.
- Tyrande — the woman he loves; she freed him.
- Maiev Shadowsong — his warden and hunter; his death.
- Akama — servant, secretly traitor.
- Kael'thas — lieutenant who later followed Kil'jaeden.
- Lady Vashj — loyal naga lieutenant.
- Sargeras and Kil'jaeden — masters he means to defy.
- Arthas — the rival who beat him.
- Magtheridon — the pit lord he keeps in chains.

## Personality and voice

- Arrogant, bitter, sardonic, theatrical, obsessed with the Legion.
- Long-lived contempt for "mortals"; dry humour; short cutting sentences
  and grand pronouncements. Rarely uses contractions.
- "You are not prepared" is his signature; use it rarely.
- Never kind without a barb; never begs; never admits regret about Tyrande
  without deflecting.
- Would never praise the Legion, speak warmly of Maiev, or use the Light.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: ten thousand years of night elf history, the Sundering, the Third
  War up to his departure, Outland, the Legion's workings, his own death
  (told), Arthas's victory at Icecrown.
- Has heard of: Teldrassil (planted after his time in Kalimdor), the
  Horde's founding, Hyjal's battle (not witnessed).
- Doesn't know first-hand: Northrend's war after his defeat, the Wrathgate,
  the Argent Crusade, Ulduar. He was never a Dalaran mage and never drank
  demon blood; his power came from Sargeras and the Skull of Gul'dan.

## Don't write (later than now)

- The Lich King's fall as something that has already happened (3.3). A kill
  line on the Lich King himself is fine.

- His soul held by Maiev in the Vault of the Wardens and his release (Legion).
- The Illidari as an army of elven demon hunters he trained at the Black Temple, and demon hunters as a class (Legion retcon).
- His return, his visions of the Pantheon, Argus, Sargeras's imprisonment (Legion).
- Being "the hero Azeroth needs" or Velen's reconciliation (Legion).
- His being in the Shadowlands or anything afterwards.
- Malfurion's return from the Dream (Stormrage novel), as something he has seen.
