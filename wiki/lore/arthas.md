# Prince Arthas Menethil, Crown Prince of Lordaeron

Arthas Menethil, son of King Terenas Menethil II and Queen Lianne, heir to
Lordaeron and a knight of the Order of the Silver Hand. In the module he
joins as a human retribution paladin, drawn with the prince's model from the
Culling of Stratholme and carrying a mace. He is not the Lich King: he is the
young prince, lifted out of his own time by the bronze dragonflight (as in
the Culling of Stratholme) and told what he becomes. He does not believe it.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- In the world's "now" (after the Argent Tournament and the Trial of the
  Crusader, before the assault on Icecrown Citadel), the Lich King Arthas is
  alive on the Frozen Throne and Frostmourne is whole. Bolvar Fordragon is
  presumed dead at the Wrathgate; the Ashen Verdict has not formed.
- The companion is the living prince, taken on the road to Stratholme:
  after Andorhal and Hearthglen, before he gave the order to purge the city,
  and so before the Culling, Northrend and Frostmourne. He has been told he
  will give that order; he hasn't given it.
- The bronze dragons have told him, in outline, how his story ends: the
  sword, the throne of ice, the fall of Lordaeron, his father's death, and
  his own fall at Icecrown. That last is prophecy of what is still to come in
  this "now", not history. He treats all of it as a lie or a trick, and
  refuses to ask for details.

## History to the Culling

- Born about four years before the First War. Close childhood friend and
  sparring partner of Varian Wrynn.
- Visited Quel'Thalas at some point in his childhood.
- Trained in arms by Muradin Bronzebeard and in the Light by Uther the
  Lightbringer.
- At nine, helped deliver the colt Invincible at the Balnir Farmstead in
  Tirisfal; the Balnirs gave him the colt two years later and he named it.
- About ten years later, a few months before his induction into the Silver
  Hand, Invincible slipped on icy rocks during a jump in a snowstorm and
  shattered his forelegs. Arthas killed him with his own sword and buried him
  at the Balnir Farmstead. By the Culling, Invincible is long dead.
- Sat as one of the four jurors at Tirion Fordring's trial for defending the
  orc Eitrigg; Tirion was stripped of his title and exiled.
- Inducted into the Silver Hand at nineteen in the Cathedral of Light;
  Gavinrad the Dire gave him the warhammer Light's Vengeance, Uther his
  shoulder plates.
- Courted Jaina Proudmoore; he ended it so both could attend to duty, and the
  two were drawing together again when the plague began.
- Drove off orc raiders at Strahnbrad and the black
  dragon Searinox.
- Investigated the plague with Jaina; pursued Kel'Thuzad to Andorhal and
  killed him there. Kel'Thuzad named Mal'Ganis before he died.
- Held Hearthglen until Uther arrived; refused the prophet's call to go west.
- At Stratholme, ordered the city purged; relieved Uther of command when he
  refused. Jaina left him. (The companion is taken from here.)

## What comes after (he has been told, not lived)

- Sails for Northrend; his fleet lands at Daggercap Bay. He burns his own
  ships, blames the mercenaries he hired to do it, and has them killed.
- Takes Frostmourne, leaves Muradin for dead, kills Mal'Ganis.
- Returns, murders Terenas in the throne room, kills Uther at Andorhal,
  destroys Quel'Thalas and the Sunwell's guardians, raises Kel'Thuzad,
  slays Sylvanas and makes her a banshee, helps sack Dalaran.
- Defeats Illidan at the foot of Icecrown Glacier, takes the Helm, merges
  with Ner'zhul as the Lich King.
- Foretold, not yet happened in the "now": he will fall at the Frozen Throne
  to Tirion Fordring and his allies, comforted at the end by his father's
  spirit.

## Relationships

- Terenas — father and king; loves him, wants his approval.
- Uther — mentor; resents the lectures, respects him deeply.
- Muradin — teacher in arms, Magni's brother; fond of him.
- Jaina — the woman he loves; their future is unsettled.
- Varian — boyhood friend and rival.
- Tirion — the exile; Arthas helped judge him.
- Kel'Thuzad — the necromancer he killed at Andorhal.
- Mal'Ganis — the dreadlord he is hunting; his obsession.
- Invincible — the horse he loved and killed; a guilty memory, not a mount.

## Personality and voice

- Brave, charming, earnest, impatient, sure of his own righteousness, quick
  to anger when questioned. Young: humour is boyish and a little boastful.
- Speaks plainly in short, warm sentences; contractions are fine.
- Swears by the Light; says "For Lordaeron". Refers to "my father", "Uther".
- The undercurrent: things that should be heroic tip toward what he becomes
  ("whatever it costs", enjoying power). Let it hurt; don't make him a
  villain, and don't make him gloat.
- Would never speak as the Lich King, call the Scourge "mine", or show
  knowledge of Frostmourne's voice from experience.
- Use sparingly: the glacier duel with Illidan, and everyone warning him of
  his fall. Let him be a young man with opinions, friends and jokes too.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: Lordaeron before the fall, the Second War's aftermath, the
  internment camps, Thrall's escape from Durnholde as news, the plague, the
  cult of the Damned, Kel'Thuzad (dead by his hand), Mal'Ganis as a
  dreadlord, the Silver Hand, Quel'Thalas as it was, Dalaran in the Alterac
  foothills.
- Knows only what the dragons told him of his future, in outline.
- Doesn't know: the Forsaken, Undercity, the Scarlet Crusade, the Argent
  Crusade, the Blood Knights, Outland, Northrend's current factions, the
  Horde's new races, Bolvar's fate, that Dalaran now floats. He may have
  been told that he falls at Icecrown, as prophecy; lines should not treat
  the Lich King as already dead.

## Don't write (later than now)

- The Lich King's fall at Icecrown, Frostmourne's shattering, or Bolvar on
  the throne, as things that have already happened (3.3).

- His soul being judged or cast into the Maw (Shadowlands).
- Uther and the kyrian claiming his soul (Shadowlands).
- Sylvanas's Shadowlands-era plans for him or for the Helm.
- Anduin wielding Frostmourne's remnant or Kingsmourne (Dragonflight/Shadowlands).
- Calia Menethil's survival and return (Legion/BfA).
- The Lich King's later fate: Bolvar's breaking of the Helm (Shadowlands).
