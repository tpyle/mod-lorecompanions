# Lore reference

Background for writing and reviewing the companions' lines in
`data/lines/`. Every line is spoken late in Wrath of the Lich King, after the
Trial of the Crusader and before the assault on Icecrown Citadel, so these
pages stop there.

- [The world, now](setting.md): who rules what, what has just happened, who
  was brought from another time, and the later lore that must stay out of
  the lines.

Each page has the same sections: where the character stands now, their
history up to it, their relationships, their personality and voice, what they
know and don't, and the later lore to avoid. Facts come from
[warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/), and each page lists its sources.

## Writing a line

1. Check it against the character's page: could they know this, and would
   they say it like this?
2. Check it against [Don't write](setting.md#dont-write).
3. A `> other:` reply is spoken by the other companion, so check it against
   their page too.
4. Add a fact you had to look up to the character's page, with its source.
