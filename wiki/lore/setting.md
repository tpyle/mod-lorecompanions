# The world, now

Every companion speaks from the same moment: late in Wrath of the Lich King,
after the Argent Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader (3.2) and before the
assault on Icecrown Citadel begins (3.3). The realm runs 3.3.5a, so the
Citadel and the Ruby Sanctum are there to be fought, but in the story they lie
ahead. This page is the shared baseline. Each character's own page (see the
[index](README.md)) adds what that character knows and how they say it.

## Where things stand

- **The Lich King lives.** Arthas sits on the Frozen Throne atop Icecrown
  Citadel, Frostmourne in hand. Tirion Fordring and the Argent Crusade are
  gathering for the assault on the Citadel; the Ashen Verdict has not yet
  been formed.
- **Bolvar is believed dead.** Bolvar Fordragon fell at the Wrathgate, and
  nobody knows that the Lich King took him. Never write a line that knows
  where he is.
- **The Wrathgate and the Battle for Undercity.** Putress's Royal Apothecary
  Society unleashed the New Plague at Angrathar, killing Bolvar's and
  Saurfang the Younger's forces. Varimathras and Putress seized Undercity;
  Thrall and Sylvanas took it back from one side, Varian and Jaina from the
  other. Varimathras and Putress are dead. The Alliance and Horde trust each
  other less than ever.
- **Northrend.** The Argent Crusade (the Argent Dawn and the Order of the
  Silver Hand, joined under Tirion) holds the Argent Vanguard and the Argent
  Tournament grounds. The Trial of the Crusader and the Trial of the
  Champion have been fought in the Crusaders' Coliseum, and Anub'arak fell
  beneath it. The Knights of the Ebon Blade, under Highlord Darion Mograine,
  hold Acherus over Icecrown. The Kirin Tor's Dalaran floats over Crystalsong
  Forest under Rhonin. The Wyrmrest Accord stands in Dragonblight.
- **Malygos is dead**, slain in the Eye of Eternity during the Nexus War.
  Yogg-Saron has been defeated in Ulduar. Onyxia has risen again in her lair.
- **The Scarlet Onslaught.** In the game's story Brigitte Abbendis died at
  New Hearthglen and Grand Admiral Westwind was then unmasked as Mal'Ganis.
  This module keeps her alive on purpose (see [her page](brigitte.md)), so
  her story is left open: she still holds New Hearthglen, and Westwind's
  secret is not yet out. Lines may suspect him; none may treat the unmasking
  as known.

## Don't write

A line is wrong if it needs any of these to be true.

- **The end of Wrath:** the Lich King's death; Frostmourne shattered; Bolvar
  under the Helm; the Ashen Verdict; Saurfang carrying his son out of the
  Citadel; Muradin and Saurfang on the gunships; Halion and the Ruby
  Sanctum. Lines may look forward to these, or fear them, but not remember
  them. (Combat and kill lines against the Citadel's bosses are fine: the
  fight is happening.)
- **Cataclysm:** Deathwing's return and the Shattering; Thrall stepping down,
  Garrosh as Warchief, Aggra and the Earthen Ring; Cairne's death; Magni
  turned to diamond and the Council of Three Hammers; worgen and the fall of
  Gilneas; Malfurion's return and his marriage to Tyrande; Sylvanas raising
  Forsaken with Val'kyr; the revamped Scarlet Monastery and Plaguelands
  (Hearthglen's fall, Tirion's fight there); Andorhal's battle; the
  Firelands.
- **Mists:** Pandaria; the destruction of Theramore; Jaina as a hawk, her
  leadership of the Kirin Tor; Garrosh's trial.
- **Legion:** Tirion's death; Varian's death; Vol'jin as Warchief and his
  death; Sylvanas as Warchief; Illidan's return and the Illidari; death
  knights raising Whitemane, Mograine, Tirion or others; Renault's betrayal
  becoming known; the class orders.
- **Later:** the burning of Teldrassil, Lordaeron's blight, Saurfang's duel,
  Kul Tiras closing its doors on Jaina, the Shadowlands (the Jailer, the Maw,
  Arthas's soul, Sylvanas's backstory and redemption, her mother as
  Ranger-General), the Dragon Isles, Dornogal.

Things that are fine: anything shown in quests, dungeons and raids up to
the Trial of the Crusader, and the novels set before it (Arthas: Rise of the
Lich King up to its Icecrown chapters, the Sunwell trilogy, Beyond the Dark
Portal, Day of the Dragon, Night of the Dragon, the War of the Ancients
trilogy, Rise of the Horde, Tides of Darkness, Lord of the Clans, Cycle of
Hatred).

## The time-displaced

Four companions were brought here by the bronze dragonflight from before
their deaths, and have been told how their stories end:

- **Prince Arthas**, from before his fall.
- **Illidan**, from the Black Temple before it fell.
- **Grand Inquisitor Isillien**, from before he struck down Taelan Fordring
  and was killed by Tirion ("In Dreams").
- **Scarlet Commander Renault Mograine**, from before Darion brought the
  corrupted Ashbringer to the Monastery and their father's spirit struck
  Renault down.

They know their own futures as hearsay, not memory, and they don't know the
world's minutiae unless someone told them or they have seen it since
arriving. Arthas's end, at the top of the Citadel, has not happened yet in
anyone's present: to him it is a prophecy everyone else is about to fulfil.
See their own pages.
