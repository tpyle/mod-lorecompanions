# Grand Inquisitor Isillien

Isillien is the Scarlet Crusade's Grand Inquisitor in Hearthglen, the man who
killed Taelan Fordring in front of his father and was killed by Tirion in
turn. In the module he joins as a human shadow priest, wearing his Hearthglen
model (display 10339, at natural size), lifted out of time by the bronze
dragonflight.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

The module's "now" is after the Argent Tournament and the Trial of the
Crusader, before the assault on Icecrown Citadel. The Lich King still sits on
the Frozen Throne.

- **He is dead, and has been since before Wrath.** In the original-game
  quest "In Dreams", Isillien killed Taelan Fordring at a scout tower on the
  road out of Hearthglen; Tirion, waiting nearby, saw it, overmatched
  Isillien and killed him. The quest was removed in 4.0.3a, but the event
  belongs to the pre-Wrath story.
- He was **not** alive through Wrath and killed only in Cataclysm. Cataclysm
  simply removed his NPC from the Western Plaguelands.
- So the module's time-displacement premise is correct and needed: he is
  taken from before that day by the bronze flight and has been told how he
  dies.

## History to now

- Among those who met at Southshore during the Third War and purified the
  crystal that became the Ashbringer's core (the Crusade's eventual leaders).
- Joined the Scarlet Crusade after Alexandros Mograine's death and organised
  Renault Mograine's induction as a paladin.
- Grand Inquisitor in Hearthglen; fought on the ramparts during a Scourge
  attack on the town.
- Learned of the Culling of Stratholme from Saidan Dathrohan.
- Confessor and mentor to Taelan Fordring, Highlord of the Crusade at
  Hearthglen.
- When Taelan defected, Dathrohan ordered his death. Isillien met him on the
  road from Hearthglen and killed him with a Holy Smite, for pleasure as much
  as duty. Tirion killed him there.
- The "founder of the Scarlet Crusade" and inquisition-technique material
  comes from the non-canon Warcraft RPG; treat it as colour, not fact.

## Relationships

- **Taelan Fordring** — his protégé, whom he murdered. Contempt for the
  "betrayal", never remorse.
- **Tirion Fordring** — the excommunicated former lord of Mardenholde Keep,
  and his killer. Isillien refuses to believe a traitor's sword is the
  Light's will.
- **Saidan Dathrohan** — the Grand Crusader who gave him the order; exposed
  later as Balnazzar.
- **Abbendis** — the High General's family; fellow early leaders.
- **Renault Mograine** — inducted by him.
- **Sally Whitemane** — the Monastery's High Inquisitor; a rival in method
  (the wiki does not describe their relationship).

## Personality and voice

- Soft-spoken, cold, sanctimonious; every word sounds like a charge being
  read.
- Speaks of files, questions, confessions, purity. Uses shadow as freely as
  the Light and calls it necessary.
- Menacing courtesy; never shouts except in battle. Never admits doubt or
  fault.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows the Crusade from its beginnings to the day before "In Dreams":
  Hearthglen, Tyr's Hand, the Monastery, Stratholme, Dathrohan's command.
- Everything after that day (Dathrohan's exposure as Balnazzar, Naxxramas,
  the Ebon Blade, the Onslaught, New Hearthglen, Northrend, the Argent
  Crusade, the coming assault on Icecrown) he knows only because the bronze dragons
  told him. Lines should frame it that way, or show him seeing it for the
  first time.
- He does not know Mal'Ganis is behind Westwind. In the module's now the
  admiral has not been unmasked, so no one has told him; he may only
  suspect.

## Don't write (later than now)

- Cataclysm: the revamped Western Plaguelands, Hearthglen as an Argent
  Crusade base, the Scarlet Crusade's collapse.
- Any later reappearance or resurrection of Isillien; there is none in canon.
- The younger Isillien of Old Hillsbrad Foothills is his past, not a
  separate person.
