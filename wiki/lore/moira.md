# Princess Moira Thaurissan (née Bronzebeard)

Moira is the only child of King Magni Bronzebeard of Ironforge and the late
Queen Eimear, and the widow of Emperor Dagran Thaurissan of the Dark Iron
dwarves. She lives in Shadowforge City, in Blackrock Depths, where she rules
for her infant son, Dagran Thaurissan II, heir to both the Bronzebeard and the
Dark Iron thrones. In the module she joins as a dwarf shadow priest, wearing
her Blackrock Depths outfit.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive, in Shadowforge City, Blackrock Depths. Her title is Princess; the
  Dark Irons know her as Moira Thaurissan.
- Her husband, Emperor Dagran Thaurissan, is dead: he was killed by the
  party Magni sent to "rescue" her. (He still appears as a Blackrock Depths
  boss; in-game kills of him are replays, not new lore.)
- Her son Dagran II has been born (around Year 25-26) and is an infant.
- Magni is still King of Ironforge, alive and not turned to diamond. She has
  not marched on Ironforge, and there is no Council of Three Hammers.
- Ragnaros still sits in the Molten Core below Shadowforge; the Dark Irons
  remain under his shadow.
- The Lich King is alive on the Frozen Throne; the Argent Tournament and the
  Trial of the Crusader are over and the assault on Icecrown Citadel has not
  begun.

## History to now

- Raised in Ironforge. After Eimear's death Magni, who had wanted a son,
  trained her as a warrior.
- Captured by Dark Iron dwarves in the Burning Steppes while travelling to
  help Lakeshire against the Blackrock orcs. Emperor Dagran Thaurissan held
  her in Shadowforge City as a potential hostage.
- She and Dagran fell in love and married.
- Magni sent a squad to bring her home. The "rescue" killed Dagran. Moira
  refused to return to Ironforge. She had announced she was pregnant around
  the time of his death.
- Ironforge (Magni, and Kharan Mighthammer among others) believes she was
  ensorcelled. She denies it.
- Gave birth to Dagran Thaurissan II.

Dark Iron background she would know:

- War of the Three Hammers (about 230 years before the Dark Portal): the Dark
  Irons under Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan fought the Bronzebeards and the
  Wildhammers. Thaurissan's wife Modgud cursed Grim Batol, the fortress the
  Wildhammers had founded, during the war; Khardros Wildhammer struck her
  down, and the curse left Grim Batol uninhabitable.
- After their defeat, Thaurissan's summoning called Ragnaros into the world,
  creating the Searing Gorge, the Burning Steppes and Blackrock Mountain.
- Second War: the Dragonmaw orcs under Nekros Skullcrusher held Alexstrasza
  captive in Grim Batol. The Dark Irons were not her jailers (the chains were
  made of dark iron, the metal); Bronzebeard fighters and Rhonin's band freed
  her. Moira should not claim Dark Iron guilt for it as fact.

## Relationships

- Magni Bronzebeard (father) — strained. She loves him and resents him; he
  sent the party that killed her husband and still thinks she was bewitched.
- Dagran Thaurissan (husband, dead) — she loved him and calls his death a
  murder.
- Dagran Thaurissan II (son, infant) — her first loyalty; heir to two
  thrones.
- Muradin Bronzebeard (uncle) — was presumed dead after Frostmourne. He
  survived with amnesia among the Frostborn (who called him Yorg Stormheart),
  is reunited with Magni and Brann during the war, and commands the gunship
  Skybreaker in the Icecrown Citadel assault. The wiki gives no date for when
  Ironforge learns he is alive, so before the assault write it as news or
  rumour ("They say he's alive"), not as an established fact.
- Brann Bronzebeard (uncle) — explorer, in Northrend's Storm Peaks and Ulduar
  during Wrath.
- The Dark Iron dwarves — her people now; she will not hear them called
  monsters.
- Ironforge court — distrusts her; she returns the favour.

## Personality and voice

- Proud, sharp-tongued, politically shrewd, quick with a put-down.
- Fiercely protective of her son and of the Dark Irons.
- Dry humour, often self-deprecating about dwarven height or her father.
- Informal register for a princess: contractions, short punchy sentences,
  "I'd", "you'll", the odd "Hm".
- Talks about ale, the Grim Guzzler, Shadowforge, the Great Forge.
- Her shadow magic is Dark Iron learning; in combat she says "Shadow take
  you" and the like rather than invoking the Light.
- She would never call her marriage a kidnapping, apologise for marrying a
  Dark Iron, or describe herself as having been enchanted.
- Use sparingly: Ironforge thinking she was bewitched or enchanted, and her
  son in the Shadowforge nursery.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: the Lich King still holds Icecrown; rumours that Muradin is alive;
  the Wrathgate and the Battle for Undercity; Ironforge politics; Dark Iron
  history and the Firelord beneath her feet.
- Knows the night elves only as the Alliance's recent allies: dwarves and
  night elves had no contact before the Third War, so no ancestral trade with
  Darnassus.
- Believes, like everyone, that Bolvar died at the Wrathgate.
- Does not know anything of Deathwing, the Twilight's Hammer's coming
  invasion of Grim Batol, or Magni's ritual in Ironforge.
- Arthas (time-displaced) has been told how his story ends; Moira knows the
  public version (Arthas took Frostmourne, Muradin was "lost").

## Don't write (later than now)

- The fall of the Lich King, Frostmourne shattered, Bolvar taking the Helm,
  the Ruby Sanctum (3.3 onward).
- Magni turned to diamond (Cataclysm).
- Moira marching on Ironforge, seizing the throne, Varian stopping her
  (The Shattering, Cataclysm).
- The Council of Three Hammers, Moira as a council member, feuds with
  Muradin over it.
- Dark Irons formally in the Alliance, as a playable allied race (BfA).
- Moira in Pandaria, the Siege of Orgrimmar, the Netherlight Temple (Legion).
- Magni as Speaker of Azeroth; Magni revived from diamond (Legion / The War
  Within).
- Moira at Amirdrassil, Dornogal, the Ringing Deeps, the Sunwell defence
  (Dragonflight, The War Within, Midnight).
- Ragnaros driven out to the Firelands, the Firelands raid (Cataclysm).
