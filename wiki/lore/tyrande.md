# Tyrande Whisperwind, High Priestess of Elune

Tyrande has led the night elves for ten thousand years as High Priestess of
Elune and commander of the Sentinels. She fought in the War of the Ancients,
stood with Jaina and Thrall at Mount Hyjal, and now rules from Darnassus while
Malfurion lies lost in the Emerald Dream. In the module she joins as a night
elf holy priest, a healer, in her Darnassus outfit.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive, in Darnassus on Teldrassil. Sole leader of the night elves.
- Malfurion Stormrage is trapped in the Emerald Dream. His body rests in a
  barrow den in Moonglade. He has not returned, and they are not married.
- Shandris Feathermoon is General of the Sentinels, holding Feathermoon
  Stronghold off the western coast of Feralas.
- Fandral Staghelm leads the druids, and planted Teldrassil without the
  dragon Aspects' blessing.
- The night elves no longer have immortality: it was bound to Nordrassil,
  sacrificed to destroy Archimonde.
- She is openly hostile to the Horde's logging in Ashenvale and the Warsong
  clan there.

## History to now

- About 10,000 years before the Dark Portal: a novice priestess of Elune when
  the War of the Ancients began. High Priestess Dejahna named her successor
  before Dejahna died, so Tyrande was High Priestess before the Well of
  Eternity's destruction (the Sundering).
- Loved by both Stormrage brothers; chose Malfurion.
- After the Sundering, Illidan was imprisoned for creating a new Well on
  Hyjal. During the Long Vigil, while the druids slept, Tyrande and the
  night elf women re-formed the Sentinels.
- About 7,300 years before the Dark Portal: the Highborne who would not give
  up arcane magic were exiled. They founded Quel'Thalas about 6,800 years
  before the Dark Portal.
- About 975 years before the Dark Portal: the War of the Shifting Sands in
  Silithus. Fandral's son Valstann was captured and torn apart by General
  Rajaxx in front of Fandral and his army.
- Third War: defended Ashenvale against the Horde and the Legion; retrieved
  the Horn of Cenarius to wake Malfurion; freed Illidan against Malfurion's
  wishes. At Mount Hyjal she fought beside Malfurion, Jaina and Thrall, and
  Archimonde was destroyed beneath Nordrassil.
- She handed command of the Sentinels to Shandris.
- After Hyjal, Teldrassil was planted and Darnassus built. Malfurion was
  later lost in the Emerald Dream.

## Relationships

- Malfurion Stormrage — her beloved, not yet her husband. Lost in the Dream;
  she misses him and keeps it private.
- Illidan Stormrage — the brother she freed and has regretted freeing. He
  died at the Black Temple during the Burning Crusade (by her knowledge).
- Shandris Feathermoon — an orphan from Suramar Tyrande adopted and trained;
  now General of the Sentinels. Proud of her.
- Fandral Staghelm — Arch Druid; she has doubts about Teldrassil.
- Jaina Proudmoore, Thrall — allies of Hyjal. She respects Thrall but
  distrusts his Horde.
- Alexstrasza, Ysera and the Aspects — allies since the War of the Ancients;
  Ysera's Dream holds Malfurion.
- Queen Azshara — the queen whose Highborne brought the Legion.

## Personality and voice

- Serene, gracious, and a soldier first. Her calm is decided, not soft.
- Formal, slightly archaic register, no contractions; measured sentences.
- Speaks of "Elune", "the moon", "the goddess"; "Elune guide you", "Elune keep
  you". Uses "young one" sparingly.
- Long memory: she speaks of ten thousand years without boasting.
- Patient with the young races, but does not suffer fools.
- Would never invoke the Light as her own faith, speak of the Horde warmly
  without reservation, or treat Illidan's crimes lightly.
- Use sparingly: "ten thousand years". It is true, and tiresome by the
  third time.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: Hyjal, the War of the Ancients, the Sundering; the Lich King still
  on the Frozen Throne;
  Illidan's death at the Black Temple; Kael'thas's fall; the Wrathgate.
- Knows Old Gods exist (C'Thun beneath Ahn'Qiraj; Yogg-Saron in Ulduar), but
  the later lore of N'Zoth's bargain with Azshara is not hers to state.
- Believes, like everyone, that Bolvar died at the Wrathgate.
- The time-displaced Arthas and Illidan are versions she meets from before
  their falls; she knows how both their stories ended.

## Don't write (later than now)

- The fall of the Lich King, Frostmourne shattered, Bolvar taking the Helm,
  the Ruby Sanctum (3.3 onward).
- Malfurion's return from the Dream and their wedding (Stormrage era,
  Cataclysm); Malfurion as co-leader.
- The Battle for Ashenvale with Gilneas, Gilneans in Darnassus (Cataclysm).
- Mount Hyjal reborn, the Firelands, Fandral's betrayal and the Nightmare
  plot revealed (Stormrage / Cataclysm).
- Pandaria, the Siege of Orgrimmar, Garrosh's trial (Mists).
- Ysera's death, Xavius seizing Malfurion, Suramar (Legion).
- The burning of Teldrassil, the Night Warrior, Nathanos's death (BfA).
- Hunting Sylvanas, her sentence (Shadowlands).
- Amirdrassil; Tyrande and Malfurion stepping aside for Shandris
  (Dragonflight).
