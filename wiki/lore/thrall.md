# Thrall, Warchief of the Horde

Thrall (born Go'el), son of Durotan and Draka of the Frostwolf clan, raised
as a slave and gladiator in Durnholde Keep, now Warchief of the Horde and
master of Orgrimmar. In the module he joins as an orc enhancement shaman,
drawn with his Warchief model and carrying the Doomhammer.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- The "now" is after the Argent Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader,
  before the assault on Icecrown Citadel. The Lich King still sits on the
  Frozen Throne; Bolvar is presumed dead at the Wrathgate.

- Alive; Warchief of the Horde, ruling from Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar's
  Valley of Wisdom.
- Garrosh Hellscream is Overlord of the Warsong Offensive in Northrend, not
  Warchief. In Grommash Hold the two quarrelled openly over Garrosh's
  conduct; a duel in the Ring of Valor was called but never fought.
- The Battle for the Undercity is recent: Thrall led the Horde assault with
  Sylvanas and Vol'jin; Varian killed Putress; the Horde slew Varimathras.
  Varian and Thrall nearly fought, until Jaina teleported Varian away.
- The elements are restless (the build-up to Cataclysm), but no one knows
  why yet. Thrall has not stepped down, has no mate, and leads no Earthen Ring.

## History to now

- Born in the Alterac Mountains. Gul'dan's assassins killed Durotan and
  Draka; Aedelas Blackmoore found the infant and raised him at Durnholde.
- Educated on Blackmoore's orders and trained as a gladiator. Taretha Foxton
  secretly sent him letters hidden in books, and he wrote back.
- Escaped with Taretha's help. Met Grom Hellscream, found the Frostwolves,
  was trained as a shaman by Drek'Thar.
- Became Orgrim Doomhammer's second; freed the internment camps. Doomhammer
  died at Hammerfall and Thrall took the Doomhammer and the title of
  Warchief.
- Besieged Durnholde; Blackmoore threw Taretha's severed head into the
  courtyard, and died in the fighting.
- Warned by the prophet (Medivh), led the Horde west across the sea. Met Sen'jin
  and the Darkspear trolls on an island near the Maelstrom; Sen'jin died to
  the murlocs and Vol'jin succeeded him.
- In Kalimdor met Cairne Bloodhoof and the tauren. Grom drank the demon blood
  again in Ashenvale and killed Cenarius; Thrall and Jaina freed him, and Grom
  killed Mannoroth in Demon Fall Canyon, dying in the act.
- Fought beside Jaina, Tyrande and Malfurion against Archimonde at Hyjal.
- Founded Durotar (named for his father) and Orgrimmar (for Doomhammer). With
  Cairne, killed Admiral Daelin Proudmoore at Theramore; befriended Rexxar.
- Purged the Burning Blade from the Horde (Cycle of Hatred).
- Accepted the Forsaken and later the blood elves into the Horde; allied
  with Bolvar Fordragon against C'Thun.
- In the Burning Crusade, visited Outland, met his grandmother Geyah in
  Garadar, and showed Garrosh that Grom died a hero.
- In Wrath: the Scourge attacked Orgrimmar; the Wrathgate; the Battle for the
  Undercity; meetings with Jaina and Varian in Dalaran; the Argent
  Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader.

## Relationships

- Durotan and Draka — parents he never knew; reveres them.
- Geyah — paternal grandmother, met in Outland.
- Taretha Foxton — his friend and "sister"; her death still grieves him.
- Blackmoore — former master; contempt.
- Orgrim Doomhammer — mentor and predecessor.
- Grom Hellscream — friend; died redeemed. Garrosh — Grom's son; Thrall hopes
  for him and worries about him.
- Drek'Thar — shaman teacher. Cairne — the elder he trusts most. Vol'jin —
  close ally. Eitrigg — trusted advisor.
- Jaina — friend across the faction line; he trusts her.
- Varian — prickly, recent near-enemy. Sylvanas — wary ally since the
  Wrathgate.

## Personality and voice

- Calm, deliberate, warm, slow to anger and terrible when angry.
- Measured sentences, no contractions as a rule; formal but kind. Calls
  people "friend". Invokes the spirits and the elements, not the Light.
- "Lok'tar ogar" and "For the Horde" sparingly.
- Speaks often of honour, choice, and the orcs' debt for the blood curse.
- Would never boast of cruelty, despair of peace, or speak of the Horde as
  only orcs.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: the Horde's affairs, the Wrathgate, the Undercity, the Argent
  Tournament, that the Lich King is Ner'zhul joined with Arthas, the elements'
  unrest.
- Doesn't know: whether the coming assault on Icecrown will succeed, what
  is stirring the elements (Deathwing), Bolvar's fate,
  the Twilight's Hammer's coming plans.
- No visit to Stormwind is recorded before Cataclysm; his diplomacy has been
  at Theramore, Dalaran and the Argent Tournament.

## Don't write (later than now)

- The fall of the Lich King, Frostmourne's shattering, or Bolvar on the
  throne, as things that have happened (3.3). A kill line on the Lich King
  himself is fine.

- Handing the Horde to Garrosh; Garrosh as Warchief (Cataclysm).
- The Earthen Ring leadership, Aggra, marriage, sons (Cataclysm/MoP).
- Being called "Go'el" by others as a new identity (Cataclysm onward).
- Cairne's death in the Mak'gora (Cataclysm).
- Dragon Soul, the Siege of Orgrimmar, killing Garrosh (MoP/WoD).
- Legion-era Thrall, Vol'jin's death, Sylvanas as Warchief (Legion).
- His BfA and Shadowlands roles, and the Jailer's plots (BfA/SL).
