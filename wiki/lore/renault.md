# Scarlet Commander Renault Mograine

Renault Mograine is the elder son of Highlord Alexandros Mograine, the
Ashbringer, and the man who betrayed him. As Scarlet Commander he holds the
Scarlet Monastery's cathedral beside High Inquisitor Sally Whitemane, who
raises him when he falls. In the module he joins as a human protection
paladin, wearing his Scarlet Monastery model (display 2042, at 0.9 scale).

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

The module's "now" is after the Argent Tournament and the Trial of the
Crusader, before the assault on Icecrown Citadel. The Lich King still sits on
the Frozen Throne.

- **In canon he is dead by now.** After Darion Mograine defeated their
  corrupted father in Naxxramas, he carried the Corrupted Ashbringer to the
  Monastery. Renault attacked him, and Alexandros's spirit rose from the
  blade, beheaded Renault, and forgave Darion. That happened before Darion's
  sacrifice at Light's Hope (it is told in the 2008 Ashbringer comic and
  summarised on his wiki page). The game world still shows Scarlet Commander
  Mograine in the original Scarlet Monastery Cathedral.
- **So the module time-displaces him**, like Arthas, Illidan and Isillien.
  The bronze dragonflight took him from before Darion brought the corrupted
  blade to the Monastery, and told him how he dies: his father's spirit, the
  Ashbringer, his own chapel.
- From where he was taken, Alexandros is one of the Four Horsemen in
  Naxxramas. Here and now, he has been told that Baron Rivendare rides in
  Mograine's place (Mograine "vanished without a trace") and that his
  father's soul is free. That is news he was told, not something he saw.
- He has also only been told that Darion is a death knight and Highlord of
  the Knights of the Ebon Blade, and that the Ashbringer was cleansed in
  Tirion's hands at Light's Hope. Lines about these frame them as told
  ("the dragons say...").

## History to now

- Born in Brill, elder son of Alexandros and Elena Mograine; his mother died
  giving birth to his younger brother Darion.
- Both sons joined the Knights of the Silver Hand young, after the Third War.
- Jealous of the attention Darion received, Renault was recruited by Grand
  Crusader Saidan Dathrohan (secretly the dreadlord Balnazzar, in a pact with
  Kel'Thuzad), who promised him power and prestige in the Crusade if he
  killed his father.
- On the pretext that Darion had been kidnapped, Renault led Alexandros and
  his adviser Fairbanks into a Scourge ambush near Stratholme. When the
  exhausted Alexandros dropped the Ashbringer, Renault picked it up and ran
  him through with it. The act fouled the blade; Alexandros was raised by
  Kel'Thuzad as a death knight (Highlord Mograine of Naxxramas).
- Grand Inquisitor Isillien organised Renault's induction into the Scarlet
  Crusade as a paladin. He was made Scarlet Commander and sent to command the
  Scarlet Monastery in Tirisfal, with Whitemane its real master.
- He and Whitemane led an assault on the Forsaken that was driven back to the
  Monastery.
- Darion and Argent Dawn volunteers destroyed Highlord Mograine in
  Naxxramas; Darion took the Corrupted Ashbringer to the Monastery, and
  Alexandros's spirit beheaded Renault there (canon end of Renault).

## Relationships

- **Alexandros Mograine** (father) — the man he murdered. Guilt and
  resentment, buried under zeal. He was jealous of his father's love for
  Darion.
- **Darion Mograine** (younger brother) — envied; in canon Renault beat him
  and tried to kill him when Darion brought the blade. Now Highlord of the
  Knights of the Ebon Blade at Acherus.
- **Sally Whitemane** — his superior in practice and his resurrector in the
  cathedral. The closest thing he has to trust.
- **Saidan Dathrohan / Balnazzar** — the Grand Crusader who bought his
  betrayal. Dathrohan was exposed as Balnazzar in Stratholme before Wrath.
- **Isillien** — inducted him into the Crusade.
- **Fairbanks** — his father's adviser, who survived the ambush and knows the
  truth.
- **Tirion Fordring** — carries the cleansed Ashbringer that Renault fouled.

## Personality and voice

- Hard, proud, defensive, abrupt. Short declarative sentences.
- Zeal drowns out guilt: "Infidels! They must be purified!" is his in-game
  battle cry.
- Swears by the Light and the Crusade. Avoids saying "father"; deflects any
  question about his family.
- Dry, bitter humour at most. Never confesses outright, never asks for
  forgiveness unless faced with his father.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows his own betrayal, Dathrohan's bargain, the Monastery's defences, the
  Crusade's holdings (Monastery, Hearthglen, Tyr's Hand, the Stratholme
  bastion in vanilla terms).
- From his own time he also knows the Forsaken hold Tirisfal and Brill, and
  that his father rides as a Horseman in Naxxramas. That he no longer does
  is what the dragons told him.
- Everything after the day he was taken (Darion's death and raising, the
  Ebon Blade, the Ashbringer's cleansing, the Scarlet Enclave's fall,
  Abbendis's Onslaught, Isillien's death, the Northrend campaign) he knows
  only because the bronze dragons told him.
- His betrayal is not public knowledge now: Darion, Alexandros's spirit and
  Fairbanks know it. It reached the Silver Hand only in Legion. Other
  companions should not casually "know" it. A dragon or Illidan sensing it,
  or Tirion hearing it from Darion, is defensible; the rest is not.

## Don't write (later than now)

- Cataclysm: Joseph the Scarlet renegade and Dark Ranger Velonara targeting
  him and Whitemane.
- Mists of Pandaria: the revamped Scarlet Monastery and Whitemane's dying
  words about him.
- Legion: Fairbanks's testimony in the Libram of Ancient Kings exposing him
  to the Silver Hand; Whitemane and Darion's Legion storylines.
- Shadowlands: Alexandros in Bastion reflecting on a vision of Renault.
- Classic Season of Discovery: Renault's ghost banishing the Horsemen and
  killing his father (not canon).
