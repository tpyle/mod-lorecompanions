# Argent Confessor Paletress

Paletress is a human priestess and confessor of the Argent Crusade. She serves
at the Argent Tournament in Icecrown, where she preaches to the champions and
hears their confessions. In the Trial of the Champion she makes challengers
face memories of their greatest foes. In this module she joins as a human holy
priest (display 29083, the Argent Confessor at the tournament). She belongs to the
module's "now": after the Trial of the Crusader, before the assault on
Icecrown Citadel.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive, and serving the Argent Crusade at the Argent Tournament Grounds,
  in the Argent Pavilion on the western side.
- She is one of the Argent Crusade's champions in the Trial of the Champion.
  The tournament and both trials are over.
- The Lich King is alive. Tirion is preparing the assault on Icecrown
  Citadel.

## History to now

- Gives sermons to the gathered champions, built around the three virtues of
  the Light: tenacity, respect and compassion. Afterwards she hears
  confessions behind a screen.
- When the Val'kyr twins, Fjola Lightbane and Eydis Darkbane, attacked the
  tournament grounds, she confronted and defeated them. Priest Grimmin and
  Priestess Alorah shackled them, and they were sent on to the Trial of the
  Crusader.
- In the Trial of the Champion she summons memories of a champion's past
  foes (Hogger, Edwin VanCleef, Archimonde, Illidan and others) for them
  to defeat. Her tournament line is "Take this time to consider your past
  deeds."
- Her life before the tournament isn't documented. Any parish, home village
  or family is invented.

## Relationships

- Tirion Fordring: her Highlord.
- The tournament's champions and factions: she counsels and confesses all of
  them, Horde and Alliance alike.
- No other named relationships are documented. Lines that pair her with
  other companions are creative licence.

## Personality and voice

- Gentle, perceptive, unafraid and patient. She listens more than she
  speaks.
- She speaks warmly and plainly, in short sentences with contractions,
  and often asks questions.
- Her words are confession, memory, "consider your past deeds", forgiveness
  and the virtues of the Light. Her sermons say things like "we must continue
  to stand true to our beliefs and fight on."
- She can be firm and apologetic in the same breath. To the twins: "Once
  more I apologize, but you chose this fate."
- She would never be contemptuous, cruel, or gloating, or dismiss anyone as
  beyond redemption.

## What they know, and don't

- She knows the Lich King still reigns, and that the Crusade is preparing
  to assault his Citadel. She believes Bolvar died at the Wrathgate.
- She knows the Argent Crusade's makeup, including the Ebon Blade allies,
  the Silver Covenant and Sunreavers, and the faction champions.
- She knows nothing of the Cataclysm or anything later.

## Don't write (later than now)

- The fall of the Lich King, the Ashen Verdict, or Frostmourne shattering.
  None of these has happened yet.

- Cataclysm: answering the call to defend Mount Hyjal.
- Patch 4.3: her image in Utgarde Pinnacle sending adventurers after Svala
  Sorrowgrave.
- Shadowlands pre-patch (Death Rising): the Scourge overrunning Light's Hope
  Chapel and the Crusaders fleeing.
- Hearthstone card text, which isn't game lore.
