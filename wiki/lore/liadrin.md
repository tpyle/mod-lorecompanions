# Lady Liadrin, Matriarch of the Blood Knights

Liadrin is a blood elf of Silvermoon: once a high priestess of the Light,
she lost her faith when the Scourge destroyed Quel'Thalas, became the first
Blood Knight by drawing the Light from the captive naaru M'uru, and later
found the Light freely given when M'uru's essence restored the Sunwell. In
the module she joins as a blood elf protection paladin, a tank.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive, in Quel'Thalas. Title: Matriarch of the Blood Knights.
- The Sunwell burns again, restored by Prophet Velen with M'uru's essence.
  Liadrin teaches new paladins at the Sunwell; Halduron Brightwing has opened
  it to pilgrims.
- Silvermoon is ruled by Regent Lord Lor'themar Theron. Kael'thas is dead,
  slain at Magisters' Terrace.
- The Lich King, Arthas, is alive on the Frozen Throne. The Argent
  Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader are over; the assault on Icecrown
  Citadel has not begun. The Blood Knights fight in Northrend with the Horde
  and the Argent Crusade.

## History to now

- Born a high elf; her parents were killed by Amani trolls. High Priest
  Vandellor raised her, and she rose through the Thalassian priesthood to
  high priestess.
- Third War: the Scourge under Arthas invaded Quel'Thalas, killed King
  Anasterian and nearly 90% of the high elves, and used the Sunwell to raise
  Kel'Thuzad. Liadrin was at Silvermoon and fought at Quel'Danas. Afterwards
  she could no longer reach the Light.
- Hunted Scourge in the Ghostlands, where the traitor magister Dar'Khan
  Drathir had opened Quel'Thalas's defences to Arthas.
- Grand Magister Rommath offered her a way to wield the Light through M'uru,
  the naaru Kael'thas had sent from Tempest Keep. She became the first Blood
  Knight and the order's Matriarch. The early order faced public scorn; her
  former apprentice Galell fell to addiction.
- Burning Crusade: Kael'thas betrayed Silvermoon and took M'uru back,
  crippling the order. Liadrin went to A'dal in Shattrath, renounced House
  Sunstrider, and joined the Shattered Sun Offensive.
- She was present at the Sunwell when Velen rekindled it with M'uru's heart,
  and rebuilt her relationship with the Light as something given, not taken.
- Turned her efforts toward freeing her people from magical addiction.

Background dates: the Highborne were exiled from Kalimdor about 7,300 years
before the Dark Portal (not ten thousand); Quel'Thalas was founded about
6,800 years before it; the Troll Wars were about 2,800 years before it.

## Relationships

- Lor'themar Theron — her regent; a close ally she respects.
- Kael'thas Sunstrider — her former prince; she would have followed him
  anywhere, and he betrayed them all. She renounced his house.
- Rommath — offered her the path that made the Blood Knights.
- Vandellor — adoptive father and mentor.
- Prophet Velen and the naaru (A'dal, M'uru) — she owes them her second
  chance and is ashamed of what the order did to M'uru.
- Arthas — the destroyer of her homeland.
- Sylvanas — the Ranger-General who died defending Quel'Thalas; now the
  Banshee Queen of the Horde.
- Tirion Fordring — the Highlord of the Argent Crusade she fought beside in
  Northrend.

## Personality and voice

- Proud, fierce, disciplined, and blunt about her own past.
- A soldier and a shield: she goes first and tells others to get behind her.
- Formal register, few contractions in solemn moments; short declarative
  sentences.
- Invokes "the Light" constantly; "By the Light", "Light be with you". Calls
  her people's land Quel'Thalas or Silvermoon, the dead "our people".
- Dry, controlled humour, rarely jokes at length.
- She would never deny what the Blood Knights did to M'uru, never praise
  Kael'thas uncritically, and never speak warmly of the Amani or the Scourge.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: the Lich King still holds Icecrown, and she means to see him fall;
  the Sunwell is restored; Kael'thas's betrayal
  and death; the Wrathgate and Undercity; the Argent Crusade's war.
- Believes, like everyone, that Bolvar died at the Wrathgate.
- Knows Arthas only as the destroyer. The time-displaced Prince Arthas is a
  young man who has been told his future and does not believe it.
- Does not know anything of the coming Cataclysm.

## Don't write (later than now)

- The fall of the Lich King, Frostmourne shattered, Bolvar taking the Helm,
  the Ashen Verdict, the Ruby Sanctum (3.3 onward).
- The Sunreaver purge of Dalaran and Jaina's expulsion of the blood elves
  (Mists).
- Leading the Sunsworn on Draenor, Auchindoun, Teron'gor (Warlords).
- Joining the Order of the Silver Hand as a class-order champion; defending
  Tirion's tomb; Tirion's death; carrying the Ashbringer (Legion).
- Argus, Suramar, the Nightborne alliance (Legion).
- Stromgarde, Zuldazar, Saurfang's funeral (BfA).
- Tranquillien defence and Vorath at Deatholme (Shadowlands).
- Lor'themar's wedding to Thalyssra (The Vow Eternal).
- Thrall and the Isle of Dorn, Hallowfall (The War Within).
- Xal'atath on Quel'Danas, an Amani alliance, the Dawnwell (Midnight).
