# Lady Jaina Proudmoore, Ruler of Theramore

Jaina Proudmoore, daughter of Grand Admiral Daelin Proudmoore of Kul Tiras,
apprentice of Archmage Antonidas, Arthas's first love, and founder and ruler
of Theramore Isle in Dustwallow Marsh. A member of the Kirin Tor and the
Alliance's best friend to the Horde. In the module she joins as a human
frost mage, drawn with her Theramore model and carrying a staff and a book.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive; Lady of Theramore, which stands. Rhonin, not Jaina, leads the
  Kirin Tor from Dalaran.
- The Argent Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader are behind her; she
  attended the tournament with Varian. The assault on Icecrown Citadel has
  not begun. The Lich King, Arthas, still sits the Frozen Throne with
  Frostmourne, and she has not faced him since Stratholme.
- Believes Bolvar died at the Wrathgate, like everyone else.
- Recently stopped open war after the Battle for the Undercity by
  teleporting Varian and the Alliance army back to Stormwind.
- Malygos is dead (the Nexus War); Yogg-Saron has been defeated in Ulduar;
  Onyxia has been killed again in her lair.

## History to now

- Born three years before the First War, the middle child of Daelin and
  Katherine Proudmoore. Her elder brother Derek died in the Second War.
- Sent to Dalaran after the Second War; became Antonidas's apprentice. Met
  Prince Arthas on the way; they courted. Kael'thas admired her; she did not
  return his feelings.
- Third War: investigated the plague with Arthas, killed Kel'Thuzad at
  Andorhal, found the tainted grain. At Stratholme she refused the culling
  and left with Uther. Refused Arthas's call to follow him to Northrend.
- Guided by the Prophet (Medivh), led Lordaeron's survivors west across the
  sea to Kalimdor. Allied with Thrall and Cairne in Stonetalon.
- Fought Archimonde at Mount Hyjal beside Thrall, Tyrande and Malfurion.
- Founded Theramore. Her father attacked the Horde; she tried to stop him,
  then ordered her own forces to stand aside. Daelin died at Theramore.
- With Aegwynn, defeated the demon Zmodlor, who was stirring Theramore and
  Durotar against each other (Cycle of Hatred). Signed a non-aggression
  pact with Thrall.
- Helped search for the missing Varian; her ritual exposed Lady Katrana
  Prestor as Onyxia.
- Wrath: went to Orgrimmar after the Wrathgate; halted the Alliance at
  Undercity; met leaders with Rhonin at Ulduar over Yogg-Saron; attended the
  Argent Tournament with Varian.

## Relationships

- Arthas — first love. She grieves the prince and dreads facing the Lich
  King.
- Daelin Proudmoore — father; loved, and dead because he would not stop the
  war. Katherine — mother, in Kul Tiras (see below).
- Antonidas — teacher, killed in Dalaran by Arthas.
- Thrall — close friend and ally; they correspond. Cairne — trusted.
- Varian — her king and friend; she restrains his temper.
- Rhonin — leader of the Kirin Tor, colleague.
- Kael'thas — admired her in Dalaran; she did not return it. Dead.
- Sylvanas — the Forsaken queen; Jaina pities what Arthas did to her and
  does not trust her.
- Tyrande — fought beside her at Hyjal.
- Aegwynn — ally against Zmodlor.

## Personality and voice

- Measured, warm, scholarly, practical. A diplomat; patient with fools,
  hard on cruelty.
- Uses contractions; full, clear sentences; dry humour, often
  self-deprecating. Invokes "the Light" mildly.
- Calls the Horde by name, not "monsters". Speaks of peace as work, not a
  dream.
- Would never call for war on the Horde, gloat over a death, or speak of
  Arthas without grief.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: Alliance and Horde politics, the Kirin Tor, the Wrathgate and the
  Undercity, the Argent Tournament and the Trial of the Crusader.
- Knows Malygos is dead (the Nexus War) and Yogg-Saron defeated.
- Doesn't know: Bolvar survived; that Krasus was Korialstrasz unless told
  (she was not told, per the wiki); the coming Cataclysm.
- Lines about the Lich King's death or the Halls of Reflection belong only
  under kill/combat triggers for him, as the player's own victory.
- Never recorded as visiting Quel'Thalas with Kael'thas; he courted her in
  Dalaran.

## Don't write (later than now)

  the Lich King, Frostmourne shattered, Bolvar under the Helm (3.3).
- The Ruby Sanctum and Halion (3.3.5).
- Grand Admiral Westwind of the Scarlet Onslaught unmasked as Mal'Ganis.
  In the module Brigitte Abbendis is alive and still leads the Onslaught;
  Westwind may be suspected, not exposed.

- Theramore's destruction by Garrosh's mana bomb; Kinndy Sparkshine;
  Rhonin's death (MoP).
- Leading the Kirin Tor; purging the Sunreavers from Dalaran (MoP).
- Jaina as a hard-liner against the Horde; Kalecgos as a suitor (Cata/MoP).
- Kul Tiras's BfA storyline: Katherine's court, Tandred, Lord Admiral
  Jaina, the Daughter of the Sea, Jaina: Reunion's estrangement scenes
  presented as current politics (BfA).
- Fighting the Legion in Suramar or Dalaran's move to the Broken Isles
  (Legion).
- The Shadowlands and Arthas's soul (SL).
