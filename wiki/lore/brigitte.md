# High General Brigitte Abbendis

Brigitte Abbendis is the daughter of the Scarlet Crusade's High General
Abbendis and succeeded him in that rank. Claiming a vision from the Light,
she took the Crusade's most devoted north as the Scarlet Onslaught and built
New Hearthglen in the Dragonblight. In the module she joins as a human holy
paladin, wearing her Onslaught model (display 24413, at 0.9 scale).

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

The module's "now" is after the Argent Tournament and the Trial of the
Crusader, before the assault on Icecrown Citadel. The Lich King still sits on
the Frozen Throne.

- **In canon she is already dead.** In the Dragonblight, adventurers sent by
  both factions killed her at New Hearthglen: the Alliance quest "My Old
  Enemy" (from Commander Eligor Dawnbringer at Wintergarde Keep, who wants
  her head) and the Horde quest "Do Unto Others", both in the game from patch
  3.0.2. Westwind knelt by her body, said "You served your purpose," and
  promised to meet the killers in Icecrown.
- **The module keeps her alive on purpose.** This is a deliberate break from
  canon, and she is not time-displaced. She still holds New Hearthglen,
  still trusts Westwind (pushing down her doubts about him), and still
  expects the Onslaught to join the coming assault on the Citadel. Lines may
  treat all of that as present and upcoming.
- What stays canon around her: Dathrohan was exposed as Balnazzar and she
  has left his Crusade behind. Isillien is dead. The Argent Crusade has
  replaced the Argent Dawn at Light's Hope.
- **Westwind has not been unmasked yet.** Because she is kept alive as she
  is, his unmasking as Mal'Ganis at Onslaught Harbor in Icecrown ("The
  Admiral Revealed") has not happened in the module's now. Lines may suspect
  him, as she and others do, but none may treat the unmasking as known. Her
  combat and kill lines where he reveals himself in front of her are fine as
  a live moment. Even then he does not die: in canon he escapes through a
  portal to Nathreza, so a "kill" line says he flees.

## History to now

- A Knight of the Silver Hand who pushed to hunt Arthas down; her father
  overruled her. When the Silver Hand split, she went with her father into
  the Scarlet Crusade, and is described as one of its original founders.
- Her father died in her arms defending Hearthglen; she succeeded him as
  High General.
- Stationed at Tyr's Hand in the Eastern Plaguelands (with Crusader Lord
  Valdelmar leading the city).
- As the Scarlet Enclave fell to the Ebon Blade's attack, she claimed
  visions telling her to take her most faithful troops to Northrend (the
  "Crimson Dawn"). She was absent from the defence, and Commander Beatrix
  presumed her dead.
- She declared the Scarlet Crusade over and renamed her forces the Scarlet
  Onslaught. A death knight disguised as a courier fed her a false message;
  her orders never reached Galvar Pureblood, whose forces were destroyed.
- Landed on the Dragonblight coast and built New Hearthglen above the
  Forgotten Shore.
- Grand Admiral Barean Westwind arrived as her adviser; his corpse was
  possessed by Mal'Ganis. She sensed something was wrong with him but
  dismissed it; the Light stopped speaking to her when he came.
- In canon, killed by adventurers at New Hearthglen (3.0). The module
  departs from this here: she lives.

## Relationships

- **Her father, High General Abbendis** — a Silver Hand leader intolerant of
  "lesser races"; he called her "Brig". She was headstrong and often defied
  him. His death at Hearthglen is the root of her grief and her new city's
  name.
- **Barean Westwind / Mal'Ganis** — her trusted admiral, in truth the
  dreadlord steering the Onslaught. Uneasy trust she never acted on.
- **Isillien** — a fellow early leader of the Crusade (the comic shows them
  together at Southshore).
- **Saidan Dathrohan** — the Grand Crusader, exposed as Balnazzar in
  Stratholme before Wrath; she left his Crusade behind.
- **The Argent Crusade, Alliance and Horde** — all enemies of the Onslaught.

## Personality and voice

- A general first: crisp, commanding, short orders ("Report.", "Hold
  formation!").
- Utterly certain of her vision, and quietly afraid of the doubt she
  dismissed about Westwind.
- A healer who treats faith as discipline and mercy as weakness.
- Swears by the Light. Little humour, all of it dry.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows the Crusade's history to the fall of the Scarlet Enclave, her own
  vision, the Onslaught's campaign in the Dragonblight and Westwind's
  presence.
- As a living "now" character she can know the Northrend war to date: the
  Wrathgate, the Battle for Undercity, the Argent Tournament and the Trial of
  the Crusader, and the coming push on Icecrown Citadel.
- She does not know that Westwind is Mal'Ganis. She has felt that something
  is wrong with him and dismissed it.
- Dathrohan was already exposed as Balnazzar before she sailed; she should
  not speak of him as a living commander she obeys.
- Isillien was already dead (killed by Tirion in "In Dreams") before Wrath.
  The Isillien in the party is time-displaced, and she knows it.

## Don't write (later than now)

- Cataclysm: the Scarlet Crusade's Plaguelands collapse, Tyr's Hand and
  Hearthglen changes (her Tyr's Hand appearance was removed in 4.0.3a).
- Her death or Westwind's betrayal stated as fact she knows. The module keeps
  her alive and unaware, though a hint or a doubt is fine.
- Classic and Caverns of Time versions (a younger Abbendis in Old Hillsbrad
  Foothills) are past, not present.
- Non-canon Warcraft RPG material.
