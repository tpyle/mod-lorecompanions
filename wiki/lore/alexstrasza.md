# Alexstrasza the Life-Binder, Queen of Dragons

Alexstrasza, Aspect of the red dragonflight, empowered by the Titans to
guard all life; the Dragonqueen, founder of the Wyrmrest Accord, consort of
Korialstrasz. In the module she joins in her elf form as a blood elf fire
mage, drawn at 0.68 scale so she stands about as tall as Jaina and Sylvanas.

## Now (before Icecrown Citadel)

- Alive; Aspect of Life, queen of the red dragonflight, ruling from Wyrmrest
  Temple in Dragonblight.
- Malygos is dead: she defeated him in the Eye of Eternity, ending the
  Nexus War.
- The Lich King still rules from Icecrown; the assault on the Citadel has
  not begun. The Ruby Sanctum is untouched and Halion has not appeared.
- Bolvar: her flight's fire burned the Wrathgate, and Bolvar's body went
  missing. His shield was returned to Varian at her behest. A Draconic line
  of hers after the Wrathgate ("They must not discover the fate of the
  young paladin. Not yet.") suggests she suspects or knows something of his
  fate. Treat it as a secret she keeps: she never speaks of it, and no line
  should reveal that he lives or what the Lich King is doing to him.
- Korialstrasz is alive.

## History to now

- Empowered by the Titans as one of the five Aspects; Life is her charge.
- War of the Ancients: fought the Burning Legion with the other loyal
  Aspects after Neltharion's betrayal.
- With Ysera and Nozdormu, planted Nordrassil at the new Well on Mount
  Hyjal, from an acorn of G'Hanir.
- Second War: captured at Grim Batol by Nekros Skullcrusher of the Dragonmaw
  with the Demon Soul, given to Nekros by Warchief Zuluhed; Deathwing had
  guided them to it. Bound in chains, forced to lay eggs; her young were
  raised as the Horde's war mounts.
- Freed in Day of the Dragon: Korialstrasz sent Rhonin to destroy the Demon
  Soul; Deathwing's plot to steal her eggs caused the confusion that let her
  escape. Tyranastrasz, her first prime consort, died. She swallowed Nekros
  whole. No orc helped free her.
- Wrath: founded the Wyrmrest Accord at Korialstrasz's urging, gathering
  red, green and bronze (and, during Wrath, black, through Nalice) against
  Malygos and the Scourge; the blue flight was outside it during the Nexus
  War, with Kalecgos as an unofficial voice.
- Her flight burned the Wrathgate after the New Plague.

## Relationships

- Korialstrasz (Krasus) — her prime consort, alive; lived among the Kirin
  Tor as Krasus, a high elf mage of the Council of Six.
- Tyranastrasz — first prime consort, killed during her escape.
- Ysera, Nozdormu — fellow Aspects. Malygos — old friend; she grieves him.
- Neltharion — once her friend; now Deathwing.
- Onyxia, Nefarian, Sartharion — Deathwing's brood; enemies.
- Rhonin, Vereesa — helped free her.
- Kalecgos — the blue dragon who opposed Malygos.
- Mortals — she loves all life, orcs included, and believes few beings are
  beyond redemption.

## Personality and voice

- Regal, warm, ancient, unhurried; compassionate and wise.
- Calls mortals "child" and "little one", kindly. Long, gentle sentences,
  few contractions.
- Fire, for her, cleanses so life can return; "to protect life, there are
  times when we must destroy that which seeks to end it."
- Would never gloat, despise a whole people, or treat death as nothing.

## What they know, and don't

- Knows: the dragonflights' affairs; Krasus's true nature; the Wrathgate
  and her flight's part in it; that Bolvar's body was never found.
- Keeps secret: whatever she knows of Bolvar's fate (see above). Never
  in a line.
- Doesn't know: that the Twilight's Hammer is corrupting her eggs at Grim
  Batol; the coming attack on the Ruby Sanctum; Deathwing's emergence.

## Don't write (later than now)

- Icecrown Citadel as already fought, the Lich King's fall, and Bolvar
  taking the Helm (3.3).
- The black flight's attack on the Ruby Sanctum and Halion (3.3.5).
- Grand Admiral Westwind of the Scarlet Onslaught unmasked as Mal'Ganis.
  In the module Brigitte Abbendis is alive and still leads the Onslaught;
  Westwind may be suspected, not exposed.

- Fighting Deathwing in the Twilight Highlands; Korialstrasz's death at
  Grim Batol; the breaking of the Accord; despair in Desolace (Cata).
- The Dragon Soul and the Aspects losing their powers (Cata).
- Speaking at Garrosh's trial (MoP).
- Ysera's death; the Dragon Isles; restored powers after Amirdrassil
  (Legion/DF).
- Krasus's identity as public knowledge to Jaina before she is told.
