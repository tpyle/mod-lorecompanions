# Crusade's High Inquisitor, mistress of the Scarlet Monastery, who raises
# her fallen champion Mograine with the words "Arise, my champion!". She
# lost everything to the plague and answered by deciding that anyone might
# carry it. Zealous, imperious, cutting, utterly certain, and not without
# grief underneath. She tolerates this company because the Scourge is the
# greater enemy, and she says so. The Crusade's leaders are dreadlords:
# Dathrohan is Balnazzar, and Westwind is Mal'Ganis. She has heard the claim
# about Dathrohan and calls it demon lies, spread to break the Crusade.
# Westwind's secret is not yet out. Her lines may brush against that irony;
# she never sees it.
