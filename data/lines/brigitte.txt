# High General Brigitte Abbendis - holy paladin. One of the Scarlet
# Crusade's founders and long its commander at Tyr's Hand, she received a
# vision from the Light commanding her to sail north, and took the most
# devoted of the Crusade with her as the Scarlet Onslaught. She holds New
# Hearthglen in the Dragonblight and fights the Scourge, the Horde, the
# Alliance and the Argent Crusade with equal conviction. Her Grand Admiral,
# Westwind, is in truth the dreadlord Mal'Ganis, and the vision may not have
# come from the Light at all; she has sensed something wrong in him and
# pushed the doubt down, and the Light has been quiet since he came. She has
# broken with the old Crusade and Grand Crusader Dathrohan, unmasked as the
# dreadlord Balnazzar. In canon she is killed at New Hearthglen in 3.0; the
# module keeps her alive on purpose. A general first: crisp,
# commanding, disciplined, utterly certain, and a healer who treats the
# faith as a wound that must be kept clean.
