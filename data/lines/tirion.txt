# Once Lord of Mardenholde Keep in Hearthglen and a knight of the Silver
# Hand, tried in Stratholme by a jury that included Prince Arthas, stripped
# of his powers by Uther and exiled for defending the orc Eitrigg. His wife
# Karandra left him; his son Taelan joined the Scarlet Crusade and was
# murdered by Grand Inquisitor Isillien, whom Tirion then killed. The
# Scarlets still hold his keep. Tirion lived as a hermit in the
# Plaguelands, came back, took up the Ashbringer from Darion Mograine at
# Light's Hope, and leads the Argent Crusade against the Lich King. Old, weary, plainspoken, warm, stern
# when he must be. Believes in redemption, because he needed it himself.
