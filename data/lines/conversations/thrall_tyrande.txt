# Thrall and Tyrande: allies of Hyjal, and everything since that has strained it.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=demon_fall_canyon
tyrande: You raised a monument here, Thrall. To the orc who killed Cenarius.
> thrall: To the friend who killed Mannoroth and freed my people from his blood. It is the same orc, High Priestess. I know.
> tyrande: Cenarius taught Malfurion everything he knew. The forest still mourns him.
> thrall: And my people still mourn Grom. I will not ask you to honour him. Only to let me.
> tyrande: ...I let you. I do not join you. That is the most I can give.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=hyjal,hyjal_summit
thrall: Hyjal. I can still hear the wisps, the night the World Tree took Archimonde.
> tyrande: The spirits of my ancestors, Thrall. They gave themselves so that you and I could stand here and argue.
> thrall: Then we should argue well.
> tyrande: Or we could, once, not argue at all. In their honour.
> thrall: ...I would like that, Tyrande.

@ trigger=idle
tyrande: Grom's son commands your army in Northrend, I hear.
> thrall: Garrosh has his father's courage, and his father's temper. I am still waiting to see the rest.
> tyrande: Then watch him closely. Ashenvale is full of young orcs who want to be Grom, and not one remembers how he ended.
> thrall: He ended a hero.
> tyrande: He ended, Thrall. That is what I am trying to tell you.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: The earth has been restless lately, High Priestess. The spirits will not say why. Do your druids feel it?
> tyrande: Some do. Fandral tells me it is nothing. Malfurion would have known.
> thrall: You miss him.
> tyrande: Every night. Do not tell anyone I said so. A High Priestess is not permitted to be lonely.
> thrall: Then I will tell only the spirits. They keep secrets better than orcs.

@ trigger=combat_start
tyrande: Stand still, Warchief. I cannot heal a target that keeps charging off.
> thrall: The Doomhammer does not stand still.
> tyrande: Then the Doomhammer can bleed. Elune guide you.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: Jaina has written to me again. She hopes we can still make peace, even after the Undercity.
> tyrande: Proudmoore always hopes. It is the bravest thing about her, and the most tiring.
> thrall: Do you share her hope?
> tyrande: I share her road. Whether it ends in peace depends on whether your Horde can keep its dead queen on a leash.
> thrall: I am watching her.
> tyrande: So is every soldier who stood at the Wrathgate. Watch harder.
