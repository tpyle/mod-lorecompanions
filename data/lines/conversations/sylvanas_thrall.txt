# Sylvanas and Thrall: the Warchief and the Banshee Queen, allies who have
# trusted each other less since the Wrathgate.

@ trigger=idle
sylvanas: Your Kor'kron followed me to the Undercity's gates again, Warchief. They are very bad at hiding.
> thrall: They are not meant to hide, Sylvanas. They are meant to be seen.
> sylvanas: So that I know I am watched.
> thrall: So that the Alliance knows I am watching. After the Wrathgate, I need them to believe it.
> sylvanas: And you? Do you need to believe it?
> thrall: I need to believe I will not have to. Help me, Sylvanas.

@ trigger=idle
sylvanas: Hellscream calls my people corpses, Warchief. In front of his warriors.
> thrall: I have spoken to him about it.
> sylvanas: Speak louder. The next time, one of my rangers may answer him with something sharper than words.
> thrall: And then I would have to answer your ranger. Do not make me choose, Sylvanas. You will not like how I choose.
> sylvanas: Then teach your Overlord some manners. It would save us both a great deal of trouble.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: When the Lich King is dead, Sylvanas, what will the Forsaken do?
> sylvanas: Survive, Warchief. We are good at that. It is all we have ever been allowed to do.
> thrall: And you?
> sylvanas: I have not looked past his death. I am not certain there is anything there to look at.
> thrall: There is. A people who need a queen and not a weapon. I hope you find them waiting.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: I remember the day Cairne brought me your envoy. Hamuul had vouched for you. I almost refused.
> sylvanas: Why did you not?
> thrall: Because my people were once hated for a curse that others laid on them. I could not hate yours for the same.
> sylvanas: A generous reason, Warchief. I came to you because I needed allies against the Scourge.
> thrall: I know. Both things can be true. That is usually how alliances are made.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=undercity
thrall: The last time I walked these halls, the floor was green with Putress's plague and Varimathras sat your throne.
> sylvanas: The halls are clean again. My apothecaries scrubbed them by hand, and I made certain they understood why.
> thrall: And the throne?
> sylvanas: I sit it myself, Warchief. Varimathras taught me what becomes of a throne left empty for an afternoon.
> thrall: Then sit it well. The Horde bled to give it back to you.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=orgrimmar
sylvanas: The last time I came to Orgrimmar, I came running, with traitors on my throne.
> thrall: You came to the Horde for help, and the Horde answered. That is what it is for.
> sylvanas: Hellscream still tells the story as though I came begging.
> thrall: Garrosh was in Northrend. I was here. I remember a queen who came to ask for an army, and got one.
