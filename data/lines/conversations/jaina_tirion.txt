# Lady Jaina Proudmoore and Highlord Tirion Fordring.

@ trigger=idle
jaina: When you reach the top of the Citadel, Tirion... will you let me speak to him first?
> tirion: If there's time. There won't be much.
> jaina: I know. I don't even know what I'd say.
> tirion: Say his name. Not the title. I'd like to know if anything in there still answers to it.
> jaina: And if it does?
> tirion: Then I'll be sorry while I do it. That's the most I can promise you.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=theramore
tirion: Theramore. Orcs on the road and nobody running. You did this, Lady Proudmoore.
> jaina: Thrall did half of it. A great many patient people did the rest.
> tirion: Took me one orc and my whole career. You've managed thousands and kept your city.
> jaina: Ask me again in ten years, Highlord. Peace is mostly paperwork and nerve.
> tirion: Then you've plenty of both.

@ trigger=idle
jaina: I'm told he came to the Coliseum himself, at the end of the Trial. Walked right in.
> tirion: He did. I told him he was outnumbered, and offered him a just death if he'd lay down the sword.
> jaina: And?
> tirion: He laughed. Then he broke the ice under the champions and dropped them to Anub'arak.
> jaina: That laugh. I used to love that laugh.
> tirion: I know, lass. I'm sorry. It isn't his any more.
