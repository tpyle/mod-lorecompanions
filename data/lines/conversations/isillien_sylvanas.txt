# Grand Inquisitor Isillien and Lady Sylvanas Windrunner.

@ trigger=idle
sylvanas: Your Grand Crusader, Inquisitor. Dathrohan. Did you know I had his own kind kill him once?
> isillien: I was told he was a dreadlord. I was not told he had been killed.
> sylvanas: I made Varimathras put a blade in Balnazzar, in Lordaeron. It did not take. The demon simply found a fresh human to wear.
> isillien: So the man who gave me my orders was a coat on a demon's back.
> sylvanas: A coat you saluted. I wonder which of us he fooled worse.
> isillien: You, Banshee. I would have obeyed him anyway.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=undercity
isillien: The Undercity. I always wondered where the Crusade's missing prisoners went.
> sylvanas: Some came here. They tell very interesting stories about your Hearthglen cells.
> isillien: I hope they were accurate. I take pride in my work.
> sylvanas: They were. My people remember everything, Inquisitor. Death is very good for the memory.

@ trigger=idle
isillien: Tell me, Banshee. When you close your eyes, what do you see?
> sylvanas: The elfgates of Quel'Thalas, Inquisitor, and a prince with a runeblade riding through them. Every time.
> isillien: That prince walks with us some days. A boy still. Does it trouble you?
> sylvanas: It amuses me. The boy has not yet done it. I have not yet forgiven it. We understand each other.
