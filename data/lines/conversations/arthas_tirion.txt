# Prince Arthas and Tirion: the juror and the exile, and the Highlord who means to face the king.

> tirion: Hated you? You were a boy, and I'd broken the law. I told the truth and took what came.
> arthas: You had a son. Taelan, wasn't it? He'd be grown now. Is he a knight?
> tirion: He was. A good one. He's dead, son.
> arthas: I'm sorry. Was it the plague?
> tirion: It was men who said they served the Light. ...Let's walk, Arthas. I don't want to talk about it today.

> arthas: I was not grinning.
> tirion: Son, I've got bad knees, not bad eyes.

@ trigger=idle

> tirion: A great deal, and most of it after your time. The short of it is that blades remember what's done with them. So do men.
