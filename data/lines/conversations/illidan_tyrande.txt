# Illidan and Tyrande: ten thousand years, a brother between them, and a promise kept carefully.

@ trigger=idle
tyrande: Illidan. When you look at the moon now, with those eyes, what do you see?
> illidan: Not the moon. A cold fire, and a great deal of magic I could take apart if I had the time.
> tyrande: That is a sad way to see the sky.
> illidan: I saw it your way once, over Suramar, when you were still a novice. You made me promise to remember it.
> tyrande: ...And do you?
> illidan: I made you a promise, Tyrande. I break those very carefully.

@ trigger=idle
illidan: Ten thousand years, and my brother has still not married you?
> tyrande: That is none of your concern.
> illidan: I was in a cage, and I still showed more initiative.
> tyrande: You showed no initiative. You had an army of demons and a very poor sense of timing.
> illidan: And yet you are still waiting for him, and I am the one here.
> tyrande: You are here because a dragon dragged you, Illidan. Do not mistake that for being chosen.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=moonglade
illidan: He is here, is he not? Malfurion. I can see the shape of him, asleep under the barrow.
> tyrande: Do not go near him.
> illidan: I was only going to look. ...He looks peaceful. It does not suit him.
> tyrande: Come away, Illidan. Whatever you meant to say to him, say it to me, and I will keep it until he wakes.
> illidan: Tell him... No. Tell him nothing. He will know.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=hyjal
illidan: My Well lies beneath that tree. I made it with my own hands, and they planted a tree on it to hide it.
> tyrande: Nordrassil was planted to seal it, and it gave us ten thousand years of immortality. We spent them to destroy Archimonde.
> illidan: Then my Well killed Archimonde. I will take that.
> tyrande: You will take anything, Illidan. That has always been the trouble.

@ trigger=idle

> tyrande: You speak of her as a joke. She gave her whole life to watching you.
> illidan: She chose that. I never asked her to stand outside my door. You, I asked. You did not come.
> tyrande: I came once, Illidan. I opened the door. Look where that led us.

@ trigger=combat_start foe=Illidan_Stormrage

> tyrande: Hold, Illidan. That one is only an echo.
> illidan: So am I. Let us see which of us is better at it.
