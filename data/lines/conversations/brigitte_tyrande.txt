# High General Brigitte Abbendis and Tyrande Whisperwind.

@ trigger=idle
tyrande: I lived under a queen, General, who trusted a counsellor with a sweet voice. Xavius.
> brigitte: And?
> tyrande: And he opened the door to the Legion, and she walked through it gladly. He never once told her no. That was her undoing.
> brigitte: Is there a lesson you want me to take, night elf?
> tyrande: Only that the most dangerous advisers do not whisper. They agree with you.
> brigitte: ...Westwind argues with me constantly.
> tyrande: Then I am glad, General. Truly.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=darnassus

> tyrande: For ten thousand years the druids slept in the Dream while we kept the watch. We grew used to it.
> brigitte: My father's knights never quite grew used to me.
> tyrande: Then they were fools, and you outlived their foolishness. Shandris would like you. She would also argue with every word you said.
> brigitte: Good. I would think less of her otherwise.

@ trigger=combat_start ctype=undead
tyrande: Elune, light our way.
> brigitte: The Light will suffice.
> tyrande: Let them both shine, General. The dead do not care whose.
