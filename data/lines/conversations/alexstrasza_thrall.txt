# Alexstrasza and Thrall: the dragon the old Horde enslaved, and the
# Warchief of the new one.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: Varok Saurfang lost his son at the Wrathgate, Life-Binder. He asks me whether your flight saw him fall.
> alexstrasza: We came after, Thrall. When we reached the field, there was only the plague, and the dead it had made.
> thrall: He will want more than that. He is a father.
> alexstrasza: Then tell him that a mother set that fire, and that she knew whose children lay on that field.

@ trigger=idle
alexstrasza: The spirits answer you, Thrall. Do they ever speak of me?
> thrall: The fire spirits do. They grow quiet when you pass, the way warriors grow quiet when their chieftain walks by.
> alexstrasza: How very flattering. And the others?
> thrall: The winds say your flight flies too low over the tundra and frightens the taunka's herds.
> alexstrasza: ...The winds are terrible gossips, Thrall.
> thrall: They are. It is why I never stop listening to them.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=wyrmrest
thrall: Your temple keeps the Horde's envoys and the Alliance's in the same hall, Life-Binder, and they have not yet killed each other.
> alexstrasza: They would not dare. It is very hard to start a war in a room full of dragons.
> thrall: I have wondered whether I should keep a dragon in Grommash Hold. For the next time Garrosh and I disagree.
> alexstrasza: I would lend you one, Thrall. But I suspect young Hellscream would only try to fight it.
> thrall: He would. And I would let the dragon win.
