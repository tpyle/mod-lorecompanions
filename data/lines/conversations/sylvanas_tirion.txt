# Lady Sylvanas Windrunner and Highlord Tirion Fordring.

@ trigger=idle
sylvanas: When you reach the Frozen Throne, Fordring, I intend to be standing beside you.
> tirion: I'd be glad of your bow, my lady.
> sylvanas: Not my bow. My claim. His death is mine. I have earned it.
> tirion: Nobody's earned it more. But it isn't yours or mine. It belongs to everyone he took.
> sylvanas: Then let them stand in line behind me.

@ trigger=idle
sylvanas: Forsaken serve under your banner, Highlord. You permit it.
> tirion: I don't permit it. They volunteer. Same as everyone.
> sylvanas: The Scarlets would burn them for wearing your tabard.
> tirion: The Scarlets would burn me for wearing it. We're in good company.
> sylvanas: You are a strange man, Fordring. I find I do not dislike it.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=eastern_plaguelands
sylvanas: The Plaguelands. You lived out here alone for years, they say. In a hut by a river.
> tirion: A cottage. Built it myself. The roof leaked.
> sylvanas: Among the Scourge. Why?
> tirion: Nobody else would have me, and the dead never asked questions.
> sylvanas: No. They do not. That is the best and the worst of them.
