# Prince Arthas and the Argent Confessor, who has seen the king he becomes.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=argent_tournament
paletress: I was in the stands at the Coliseum when he came, Arthas. The Lich King. He broke the floor out from under the champions.
> arthas: What did he look like?
> paletress: Tall. Cold. He spoke with your voice, but older, and very tired of everything.
> arthas: ...Why are you telling me?
> paletress: Because you asked, in your way. You've been looking toward his Citadel since we got here.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: In the Trial I call up memories for the champions to face. Hogger. VanCleef. Archimonde. Even Illidan.
> arthas: Never me?
> paletress: Never you. I'm not sure the Light would let me. And I'm not sure which one would come.
> arthas: The prince or the king.
> paletress: Yes. I'd like to go on thinking it might be the prince.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Your sermon. Tenacity, respect, compassion. Uther gives the same one, only longer.
> paletress: Which of the three do you find hardest?
> arthas: Respect. When people are wrong, I want to tell them. Loudly.
> paletress: Then we'll start with respect. You've tenacity to spare, and compassion we can find. Shall we work on it together?
> arthas: You sound like a schoolmistress.
> paletress: I'll take that. Schoolmistresses get results.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Jaina's alive, here. Older. Ruling a city across the sea. Did she ever... marry?
> paletress: Not that I've heard. You could ask her yourself, you know.
> arthas: And say what? Sorry about the thing I haven't done yet?
> paletress: That's a fine start. Most of the men who come to my tent can't manage half of it.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: Is there something you've never told anyone, Arthas? Something old?
> arthas: There was a horse. Invincible. I pushed him to jump in a snowstorm, and he broke his legs on the ice. I put him down myself.
> paletress: And you've carried that since.
> arthas: It was my fault. I wanted the jump. He'd have done anything I asked.
> paletress: Then learn this from him. Love that asks too much stops being love. Learn where that line is, Arthas, before it costs you more than a horse.

@ trigger=quest_complete
arthas: Done! Clean, quick, and nobody hurt.
> paletress: Well done. Though you were rather enjoying yourself near the end.
> arthas: Is that wrong?
> paletress: Not today. Just notice it. That's all I'll ever ask.
