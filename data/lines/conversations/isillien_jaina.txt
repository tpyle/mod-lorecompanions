# Grand Inquisitor Isillien and Lady Jaina Proudmoore.

@ trigger=idle
isillien: You conjure bread from the air, Lady Proudmoore. In Hearthglen we burned women for less.
> jaina: In Dalaran we call it breakfast. It's a first-year spell, Inquisitor.
> isillien: So was necromancy, I expect, for Kel'Thuzad. Your city taught him both.
> jaina: And cast him out for the second. We were too slow. I know that better than you ever will.
> isillien: A confession, my lady. I shall file it under "promising".

@ trigger=zone_enter where=dalaran
isillien: Dalaran, in the sky. Your Kirin Tor have always floated a little above the questions, Lady Proudmoore.
> jaina: We answer plenty of questions. We just don't do it with hot irons.
> isillien: A pity. Antonidas would have made a fascinating subject. I hear he did not survive the prince.
> jaina: He didn't. And if you say his name in that tone again, you'll learn exactly how far down it is from up here.
> isillien: Noted, my lady. I shall save the tone for people who cannot fly.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=theramore
isillien: Theramore. Humans and orcs sharing a harbour. I count three separate heresies from here alone.
> jaina: Only three? You're slipping.
> isillien: I am being polite. I am told you are well liked here.
> jaina: I am. It's a strange feeling, being liked. You should try it sometime.
> isillien: I tried it once. I did not care for the company.
