# Tyrande and Whitemane: ten thousand years of patience against a young woman's fire.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: Your people lived forever, I am told. That is a heresy against the order the Light ordains.
> tyrande: We gave it up, Inquisitor. Willingly. To destroy a demon who would have ended your world.
> whitemane: Then the Light made you mortal to humble you.
> tyrande: No. We made ourselves mortal to save you. You may thank us or not. Elune does not keep accounts.

@ trigger=idle
tyrande: You are very young to carry so much anger, Inquisitor. Who did you lose?
> whitemane: Everyone. My whole family. I burned them myself when they rose.
> tyrande: Then I am sorry. I have buried more sisters than you have met people. It never grew easier. Only quieter.
> whitemane: I do not want it quieter, night elf. I want it purged.
> tyrande: I know. That is what frightens me for you.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: You are no friend of the Horde, night elf. Help me purge the Forsaken from Tirisfal, and your moon may share the glory.
> tyrande: I do not trust the Horde. I do not trust their dead queen. And still I do not burn villages, Inquisitor.
> whitemane: Then you will lose to those who do.
> tyrande: I have outlived a great many people who told me that.
