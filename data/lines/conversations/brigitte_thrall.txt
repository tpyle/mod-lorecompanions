# Thrall and High General Brigitte Abbendis of the Scarlet Onslaught.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: General. Your admiral, Westwind. My shamans in the Dragonblight say the spirits go quiet around him.
> brigitte: Your shamans talk to rocks, orc.
> thrall: And the rocks are seldom wrong. You have felt it too. I see it in your face when his name comes up.
> brigitte: ...The Light has been quiet. That is a trial of faith, not a sign.
> thrall: Then I hope your faith is stronger than my rocks. Truly.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=wrathgate
thrall: The Wrathgate. Saurfang's son died here, with Bolvar and thousands more.
> brigitte: I know. We heard of it at New Hearthglen within the day.
> thrall: Would your Onslaught have helped them?
> brigitte: We were not asked, and they would not have accepted. Still. I prayed for Fordragon. He was a paladin, whatever banner he stood under.
> thrall: Then we have that in common, General. I asked the spirits to guide him home.

@ trigger=revived
thrall: The spirits sent me back.
> brigitte: The spirits had help. No one under my command stays down.
> thrall: Not even an orc?
> brigitte: Do not push it. On your feet, Warchief. Report.
