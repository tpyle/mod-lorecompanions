# High General Brigitte Abbendis and Princess Moira Thaurissan.

@ trigger=idle
brigitte: My clerks tell me you returned the Onslaught's letter, Princess. In ashes.
> moira: Thought you'd want it back. Waste not.
> brigitte: The next letter will be sealed with lead.
> moira: Lovely. Lead doesn't burn, but it melts beautifully at the Black Forge.
> brigitte: You are very tiresome.
> moira: I'm a princess, General. It's most of the job.

@ trigger=idle
brigitte: My father never once praised me in front of his knights. He said it would make them think less of me.
> moira: Mine put an axe in my hands because he'd wanted a lad. I was the son he didn't have.
> brigitte: Did it make him proud?
> moira: For a while. Then I married someone he didn't choose, and he sent soldiers to fetch me home. You?
> brigitte: He died before I had the chance to disappoint him.
> moira: Hm. Not sure which of us got the worse bargain.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=blackrock_depths
brigitte: The Dark Iron capital. I can smell the sulphur in my armour.
> moira: That's the Firelord's breath. He's under our feet. Mind you don't wake him with your praying.
> brigitte: You hold court above Ragnaros himself, and you call that ruling?
> moira: I call it a lease. The Firelord's only a lodger, General. We'll evict him one day.
> brigitte: The Light could help you with that.
> moira: The Light's welcome to try. Tell it to bring a bucket.
