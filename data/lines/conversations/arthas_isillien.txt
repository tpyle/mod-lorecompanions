# Prince Arthas Menethil and Grand Inquisitor Isillien, both time-displaced.
# Arthas comes from the road to Stratholme, before the purge, and does not
# know the Scarlet Crusade; Isillien comes from years after the Culling, and
# admires it.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=hearthglen
arthas: Hearthglen. I held these walls only days ago. Days for me, I mean. I waited here for Uther.
> isillien: And Uther came, and lectured you, I expect. He lectured everyone.
> arthas: He did. ...Whose banners are these? Red. I don't know them.
> isillien: Mine, my prince. The Scarlet Crusade. We are what your Lordaeron leaves behind.
> arthas: Then Lordaeron should have left something kinder.

@ trigger=idle
isillien: You sat on Fordring's jury, my prince. It is the one verdict of yours I would have framed.
> arthas: I voted with the others. He'd sheltered an orc. The law was clear.
> isillien: It was. And now the dragons say Fordring is the man who ends you, at the top of a tower of ice.
> arthas: The dragons say a lot of things.
> isillien: They say he ends me too, on a road out of Hearthglen. Exiles keep coming back, my prince. I would not exile the next one.
> arthas: What would you do instead?
> isillien: Something more permanent.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=stratholme,stratholme_city,culling_of_stratholme
isillien: Stratholme. Dathrohan told us what you did here. Half the order wept. The other half took notes.
> arthas: Which half were you in?
> isillien: I have never wept, my prince.
> arthas: I haven't done it. Not yet. The grain's in their bellies, but I haven't...
> isillien: You will. And some of us will call it the bravest thing a prince of Lordaeron ever did.
> arthas: Don't. Don't you dare make me into your saint.

@ trigger=idle
isillien: You keep looking at the road behind us, my prince. Waiting for someone?
> arthas: No.
> isillien: Lady Proudmoore leaves you at Stratholme, in every account I have read. She does not argue for long.
> arthas: Keep her name out of your mouth.
> isillien: As you wish. But I have a file on everyone, my prince. Yours is the only one with a sequel.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Fordring had a son, didn't he? Taelan. He'd be grown, by your time.
> isillien: He is my pupil. The dragons say he will turn traitor, and that I will kill him for it on the road.
> arthas: You'd kill your own student?
> isillien: You are told you will kill your own teacher, my prince. Uther, at Andorhal. Shall we compare our reasons?
> arthas: I'd never... Light, I'd never.
> isillien: Taelan would say the same, I expect. The dragons tell me he says it all the way down the road.

@ trigger=combat_start foe=mal'ganis
arthas: Mal'Ganis! You're mine!
> isillien: So that is a dreadlord. Dathrohan never introduced me to his friends.
> arthas: Less talk, Inquisitor!
