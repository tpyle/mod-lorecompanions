# Scarlet Commander Renault Mograine and Highlord Tirion Fordring.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=blackrock_spire
tirion: Blackrock Spire. I killed an ogre here once, just as it swung at your father. Did he ever tell you?
> renault: Every winter. He made it sound as if you had saved the kingdom.
> tirion: He made everything sound like that. In his version, I'm taller.
> renault: In his version you were a giant. I hated you a little, as a boy.
> tirion: ...Only a little? I'll take it.

@ trigger=idle
tirion: Darion told me some things at Light's Hope, Renault. Not everything.
> renault: What things.
> tirion: That your father was proud of you both. That he'd have said so more, if he'd known how.
> renault: That is not what Darion told you.
> tirion: No. It's what I'm telling you. The rest stays between me and the Light.
> renault: ...Then keep it there, Fordring. I am asking you.

@ trigger=idle
tirion: Here. Hold it. Just for a moment.
> renault: Put it away, Fordring.
> tirion: It's clean now, son. It won't hurt you.
> renault: You do not know that. You do not know what it remembers.
> tirion: I know it remembers your father. That ought to count for something.
> renault: It does. That is exactly the problem.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=brill
renault: Brill. I was born here. Now the dead keep the inn.
> tirion: They do. The beds aren't bad.
> renault: You have slept in a Forsaken inn.
> tirion: I've slept in worse. Your mother used to bring Alexandros his supper out to the yard here, and scold us both for being late.
> renault: ...I do not remember her voice. I remember the scolding. That is something.

@ trigger=combat_start ctype=undead
tirion: Renault, shield up. Left side.
> renault: I know where my shield goes, Fordring.
> tirion: Your father said the same. Left side, son.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=naxxramas
renault: Naxxramas. My father rides for the Lich King in there. Or he did, the dragons say.
> tirion: He did. Darion and the Argent Dawn put him down here, and Darion freed his soul at Light's Hope. It cost him his life.
> renault: Of course it was Darion. It was always going to be Darion.
> tirion: Would you have wanted it to be you?
> renault: ...The dragons say I had my chance. Leave it, Fordring.
