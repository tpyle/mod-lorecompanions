# Liadrin and Paletress: courage, blame, and the three virtues.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: You had an apprentice once, didn't you, Matriarch? Galell.
> liadrin: He fell to the hunger. I could not hold him. I taught him everything except how to stop.
> paletress: Do you think he blames you?
> liadrin: I do not know. I blame myself. That is enough for both of us.
> paletress: It isn't, you know. Blame doesn't share well. Someday you should give him back his half.

@ trigger=idle
liadrin: I saw you face the Val'kyr twins, Confessor. My knights still argue over whether it was courage or madness.
> paletress: Neither. They'd come to hurt people who'd come to me for comfort. I wasn't going to let them.
> liadrin: That is courage, then.
> paletress: It's a confessor's job. You'd be surprised how much of courage is just not leaving the room.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: Tenacity, respect, compassion. Which of the three is hardest for you, Matriarch?
> liadrin: Respect. Tenacity I learned from the Scourge. Compassion I learned from a naaru.
> paletress: And respect?
> liadrin: I am still learning it. Mostly from people like you, who give it before it is earned.
> paletress: That's the only way it ever gets earned, I think.
