# Alexstrasza the Life-Binder and Highlord Tirion Fordring.

@ trigger=idle
alexstrasza: Your blade burns the dead, paladin. It burns the way my flight's fire does: to clear the ground.
> tirion: I hadn't thought of it like that.
> alexstrasza: Fire is not only an ending, Tirion. After the burning, the grass returns.
> tirion: After this war, I'd like to see some grass. Plain, green, and nothing on fire.
> alexstrasza: Then I will find you a hill of it, when this is done.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=wrathgate
tirion: The Wrathgate. Your flight burned it clean after the plague, Life-Binder.
> alexstrasza: We did. Nothing was left alive there to save, and much lay there that should not rise again.
> tirion: That can't have been an easy order to give.
> alexstrasza: It was the hardest I have given in an age, child, and the quickest.
> tirion: I've given a few like it. You don't get any faster at them.
> alexstrasza: No. Only quieter.

@ trigger=idle
alexstrasza: You call yourself old, Tirion. You say it often.
> tirion: Because it's true. My knees have opinions.
> alexstrasza: I was old when your kingdoms were forest and marsh. You are a fledgling.
> tirion: Well. That's the nicest thing anyone's said to me all war.
> alexstrasza: Then sit a while, fledgling. The Citadel will wait an hour.
