# Thrall and the Argent Confessor.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: Warchief, may I ask whose name you say before you sleep?
> thrall: How do you know that I say one?
> paletress: Your lips move. I've heard confessions a long time.
> thrall: Taretha. A human girl who freed me, and died for it. I never thanked her as I should have.
> paletress: You say her name every night. I think she's been thanked.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: Your Overlord Hellscream has never once come to my tent.
> thrall: Garrosh does not believe he has anything to confess.
> paletress: Those are usually the ones with the most.
> thrall: I know, Confessor. I fear I will be the one who hears it one day, and I will not like it.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=argent_tournament
thrall: The tournament grounds. Orcs and humans breaking lances on each other for sport, and everyone goes home alive.
> paletress: Mostly alive. I've set a few collarbones.
> thrall: It is more peace than the Alliance and the Horde have managed in a generation.
> paletress: Then let's not tell anyone it's working. They'd only spoil it.
