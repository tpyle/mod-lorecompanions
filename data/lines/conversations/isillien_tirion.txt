# Grand Inquisitor Isillien and Highlord Tirion Fordring.

@ trigger=idle
isillien: The dragons say you kill me on the Hearthglen road, Fordring. Over the boy.
> tirion: I did. I'm not going to do it twice.
> isillien: How generous. Or is it that you cannot?
> tirion: It's that I'm tired, Isillien. Killing you didn't bring him back the first time.
> isillien: Perhaps it would be more satisfying with practice.
> tirion: Walk ahead of me. I'd rather see where you are.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=mardenholde
isillien: Mardenholde. Your old keep, Fordring. The Crusade keeps it better than you did.
> tirion: You hang men from my battlements.
> isillien: Only the guilty.
> tirion: And who decides that?
> isillien: I do. That is rather the point of me.

@ trigger=idle
tirion: The order to kill my son came from Dathrohan. Dathrohan was Balnazzar. You took your orders from a demon, Isillien.
> isillien: So the dragons tell me. I find it changes nothing.
> tirion: Nothing?
> isillien: A traitor is a traitor, whoever signs the warrant. Taelan would have died by my hand with or without a dreadlord's blessing.
> tirion: ...You know, I think that's the first honest thing you've ever said to me.
> isillien: I am always honest, Fordring. It is why people dislike me.

@ trigger=combat_start ctype=undead
isillien: The dead. Shall we, Fordring? I will try not to mistake you for one of them.
> tirion: Keep your shadow off my back.
> isillien: I never strike from behind. I like them to see me.

@ trigger=idle
tirion: Paletress says no one's beyond redemption. I've been trying to believe it about you.
> isillien: And?
> tirion: Some days I manage. Today isn't one of them.
> isillien: Then we understand each other. I have never once tried to believe it about you.
> tirion: That's the difference between us, Isillien. I try.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=hearthglen
tirion: Hearthglen. The tower's just down that road.
> isillien: I know the tower. In my time, I have not yet walked to it.
> tirion: You will. Everyone keeps telling me the dragons don't lie.
> isillien: Then I will meet him there, and you will be late. You are always late, Fordring.
> tirion: ...Not this time. This time I'm walking beside you, and you're going nowhere near that road.

@ trigger=idle
isillien: You heal with the Light and kill with it. I do both with shadow. Our sermons differ only in the lighting.
> tirion: The Light asks me to be sorry when I kill. Does yours?
> isillien: Mine asks for a report, in triplicate.
> tirion: ...Light help me, that was very nearly funny.
