# Thrall and Scarlet Commander Renault Mograine, who does not talk about his family.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=tirisfal
renault: Tirisfal. Your Forsaken hang their lanterns in the windows of houses I grew up in.
> thrall: They are not mine, Mograine. They are their own. They chose the Horde, and the Horde chose them.
> renault: You chose corpses.
> thrall: I chose people who had nothing left but their will. I know something about that.
> renault: You know nothing about it, orc. Walk on.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: You do not speak of your family, Mograine. Every human here speaks of their family, sooner or later.
> renault: Not every human.
> thrall: Your brother, then. Darion Mograine is your brother, is he not?
> renault: Darion. A death knight now, they tell me. The Lich King's hound off its leash.
> thrall: Highlord Darion sent his knights to Orgrimmar, and I took them into the Horde. He is no hound. He chose his side.
> renault: Then he chose better than I did. That would be a first. Drop it, orc.

@ trigger=combat_start
renault: Infidels! Stand with me or stand aside, orc!
> thrall: I stand with you, Mograine. Mind your flank.
> renault: Fine. Do not make me save you.
