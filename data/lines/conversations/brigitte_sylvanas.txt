# High General Brigitte Abbendis and Lady Sylvanas Windrunner.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=venomspite
sylvanas: Venomspite. My outpost, General, and a short ride from your walls. We watch you.
> brigitte: And we watch you. My scouts count your apothecaries every morning.
> sylvanas: Count carefully. I have recently had trouble with apothecaries myself.
> brigitte: Putress. At the Wrathgate. Your own man.
> sylvanas: Was. Varian's soldiers saw to him in my Undercity. For once the Alliance did me a kindness.

@ trigger=idle
brigitte: I argued to hunt Arthas down, Banshee, before he reached your forests. They told me no.
> sylvanas: I was still alive when they told you no, I expect. I have often wished someone had listened.
> brigitte: A compliment. From you.
> sylvanas: A fact, Abbendis. Do not grow fond of it. When he is dead, your Onslaught and my Forsaken will have nothing in common.
> brigitte: We have nothing in common now.
> sylvanas: Then why do you keep looking at Icecrown the way I do?

@ trigger=zone_enter where=ruins_of_lordaeron,undercity
brigitte: The capital. I rode through these ruins once, scouting your kind. You were fewer then.
> sylvanas: And you were more, General. Time is kinder to the dead than to crusades.
> brigitte: This was the capital of Terenas Menethil, and you rule beneath it like a crypt-spider.
> sylvanas: I leave it up there in the rubble, where Arthas left his father. I keep it as a reminder.
> brigitte: Of what?
> sylvanas: That kings die, General. Even the ones who are sure the Light is with them.
