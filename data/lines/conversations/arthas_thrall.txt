# Prince Arthas and Thrall: the slave who escaped and the heir who never knew.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Warchief, the camps. We were told the orcs were sick. A lethargy. That we were keeping you until you got better.
> thrall: We were sick, Arthas. The demons' fire had gone out of our blood and left us hollow. Your people saw a sickness and built a cage around it.
> arthas: Father said the camps were cheaper than another war.
> thrall: They were. For you.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Can I ask you something? Why keep the name? Thrall. It's a slave's name.
> thrall: It was given to me in Durnholde, and I will not let myself forget Durnholde.
> arthas: I'd have thrown it in the sea.
> thrall: Then you would have had to find another way to remember. I have found none better.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: They say you were Blackmoore's gladiator. Never beaten. Fancy a bout tonight? Wooden weapons, nothing serious.
> thrall: I fought in that ring for men who called me a beast. I swore I would never fight for show again.
> arthas: Not for show. For fun. There's a difference.
> thrall: ...Very well. But I will not let you win because you are a prince.
> arthas: Good. Muradin always does, and swears he doesn't.

@ trigger=idle
thrall: The Scourge came to Orgrimmar before the Horde sailed north. Plagued grain in crates, and then the dead in our streets. They came in your name, Arthas.
> arthas: Grain. That's how Kel'Thuzad did it in Lordaeron. ...That isn't me. That's a thing wearing my face.
> thrall: I know. I fought it. And now here you are, a boy who asks me questions no human has ever asked.
> arthas: Disappointed?
> thrall: Relieved. I did not expect to like a Menethil.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=durnholde
thrall: Durnholde. I spent my childhood behind those walls. Most of it in a cell.
> arthas: I knew Blackmoore's name. Everyone at court did. They said he drank.
> thrall: He did. A girl named Taretha Foxton sent me letters hidden in books, under his roof. He killed her for helping me escape.
> arthas: ...I didn't know that.
> thrall: No one at your father's court asked. Remember her, prince, if you are ever king.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=orgrimmar
arthas: You built this? In a desert? I thought orcs only knew how to burn things.
> thrall: Orcs learnt to burn things from demons. We are learning to build from each other. It is slower.
> arthas: It's... honestly, it's magnificent. Don't tell my father I said that.
> thrall: Your secret is safe with me, prince. Very little reaches the Alliance from here these days.

@ trigger=combat_start
arthas: For Lordaeron!
> thrall: Lok'tar ogar!
> arthas: Is that the same thing?
> thrall: Near enough. Fight!
