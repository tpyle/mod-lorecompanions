# Scarlet Commander Renault Mograine and High Inquisitor Whitemane.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=hillsbrad,southshore,old_hillsbrad
whitemane: Hillsbrad. Do you remember, Mograine? You were the champion, and Jimmy was the dragon.
> renault: Jimmy was always the dragon. He liked to lose.
> whitemane: And you died beautifully. Every time.
> renault: And you raised me. Every time. Some things do not change, Sally.
> whitemane: Some things should not.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: The dragons told you how you die, Mograine. You have not told me.
> renault: No.
> whitemane: I raise you. If there is a death I cannot undo, I have a right to know it.
> renault: There is. You cannot undo it. That is all you get.
> whitemane: ...Then I will be there regardless. Whatever the dragons say.
> renault: Sally. Please. For once in your life, do not.

@ trigger=idle
renault: When you raise me, you always say the same thing. "Arise, my champion."
> whitemane: It works. Why would I change it?
> renault: You could try "Get up, you oaf." Just once.
> whitemane: You would lie there out of spite.
> renault: ...I would, yes.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: Your brother leads the death knights at Acherus now. Does it grieve you?
> renault: I do not have a brother.
> whitemane: You had one when we were children. He trailed after us everywhere, and you sent him home every time.
> renault: Then he learned early. Leave it, Sally.
> whitemane: As you wish. But I remember him, even if you will not.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=scarlet_monastery
renault: The Monastery. Home, such as it is.
> whitemane: Such as it is? I have kept it for you, Mograine. Every stone.
> renault: I know. I just do not care for the chapel any more.
> whitemane: You have prayed in that chapel for years.
> renault: The dragons told me something about it. Do not ask me what.
> whitemane: ...Then I will pray in it for both of us.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: Little Jimmy asked after you, before I came away. He sends his regards.
> renault: Vishas? He still questions the prisoners in the graveyard?
> whitemane: He has a gift for it. I would not have guessed it of the boy who wept at our pretend funerals.
> renault: We all became what we pretended to be, Sally. You raise me. I fall down.
> whitemane: And Jimmy makes people talk. Childhood is a cruel prophet.
