# Argent Confessor Paletress and Lady Sylvanas Windrunner.

@ trigger=idle
sylvanas: Your arena raises the memories of a champion's enemies, priestess. Could it raise Arthas?
> paletress: Only the Arthas you remember. Not the one on the throne.
> sylvanas: I remember him very well. I would kill the memory. Again and again.
> paletress: I won't do it, Lady Sylvanas. It wouldn't feed you. It would only feed him.
> sylvanas: Everything feeds him, priestess. That is why he must die.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: Some of your people come to my pavilion, you know. Forsaken.
> sylvanas: To confess? To you?
> paletress: Mostly to talk about who they were. A baker. A fisherman. A schoolteacher from Brill.
> sylvanas: ...And you listen.
> paletress: Every word. Someone ought to remember them both ways.
> sylvanas: Do not expect me to thank you, priestess. But do not stop.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: You have sisters, haven't you?
> sylvanas: Had. One went through the Dark Portal and never came back. One wears the Silver Covenant's colours in Dalaran.
> paletress: Vereesa. I met her once. She stands very straight whenever a Sunreaver walks past.
> sylvanas: She always did. Even as a child, when she was angry.
> paletress: You smiled, just now.
> sylvanas: You are mistaken, priestess. The dead do not smile.
