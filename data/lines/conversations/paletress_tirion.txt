# Argent Confessor Paletress and Highlord Tirion Fordring.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: I still think about the Black Knight. The herald never saw him coming.
> tirion: He was a message, Paletress. Arthas wanted us to know he could reach into the Coliseum.
> paletress: He reached in twice. The second time he came himself.
> tirion: And the champions went down into the pit after Anub'arak and came back up. That's our message.

@ trigger=idle
paletress: You never come to my screen, Tirion. Everyone else does.
> tirion: I'm the Highlord. I'd hate to frighten the recruits by joining the queue.
> paletress: That's a joke. You make jokes when you're hiding something.
> tirion: ...There's a boy on a road outside Hearthglen. I see him most nights. I don't need to confess it. I need to stop dreaming it.
> paletress: Then come anyway. Sometimes we just sit with them a while. The dreams get lighter.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=zuldrak

> paletress: He came to my screen once, you know.
> tirion: Did he? I'd never have taken him for the confessing sort.
> paletress: I can't tell you what he said. I can tell you he left lighter than he came.
> tirion: ...Good. He's carried enough for the both of us.

@ trigger=idle

> paletress: A village chapel. Small. Farmers, mostly. They didn't have much to confess but the weather.
> tirion: Farmers confess the worst things. I was one, in the exile years. You don't want to hear what I said to that horse.
> paletress: I'm sure Mirador forgave you.
> tirion: He bit me twice a week. That's how I knew.

@ trigger=idle
tirion: Lightbane and Darkbane. You beat them both on the tournament grounds, and I never thanked you properly.
> paletress: You sent them on to the Coliseum in chains. I'm still not sure that was kind.
> tirion: Kinder than what the Lich King would have done with them.
> paletress: I apologised to them, you know. Before the shackles.
> tirion: Of course you did.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=icecrown_citadel

> tirion: Arthas? I've thought about it for months. Nothing I come up with is worth saying.
> paletress: Then don't make a speech. Tell him the truth. It's what you do.
> tirion: The truth is I'm sorry for the boy and I'll kill the man. Doesn't fit on a banner.
> paletress: It fits on you. That's enough.
