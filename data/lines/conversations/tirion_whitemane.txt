# Highlord Tirion Fordring and High Inquisitor Whitemane.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: I stood among the witnesses when your son was inducted into the Crusade, Fordring. Did you know that?
> tirion: No. I didn't. ...How did he look?
> whitemane: Proud. Certain. Like a man who had found his father's faith without his father.
> tirion: Then he found it somewhere better than me. I'd have liked to give it to him myself.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=hearthglen
tirion: Hearthglen. My boy learned to ride in that square, before the exile. Before your Crusade.
> whitemane: It is where we hang plague-carriers now, Fordring.
> tirion: I know. I've watched from the hills. Some of them weren't sick, Whitemane.
> whitemane: Some of them were. That is the arithmetic of this land, and I did not write it.
> tirion: No. But you keep checking the sum, like you're afraid it's wrong.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: Your Crusade marches beside the Forsaken. You let them wear the Argent tabard.
> tirion: I let anyone wear it who'll stand between the Lich King and the living.
> whitemane: They are the plague in armour, Fordring.
> tirion: They're what the plague left behind. So are you, Whitemane. So am I. We're all leftovers.
> whitemane: Do not presume to put me in their company.
> tirion: I'm not. I'm putting them in yours. There's a difference, and one day you'll see it.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: You are limping, Fordring.
> tirion: Knees. They've been carrying armour since before you were born.
> whitemane: I could heal them.
> tirion: Would you? That's the kindest thing you've ever said to me.
> whitemane: Do not mistake it. I need you upright until the Lich King is dead. After that, limp as you please.
> tirion: Ha. There's the Whitemane I know.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: Fordring. You believe anyone can be redeemed. Even the dead.
> tirion: I believe the Light doesn't give up on people as quick as we do.
> whitemane: My family rose. All of them. I put them down with my own hands. Tell me they could have been redeemed.
> tirion: ...No. Not those. The plague took them before they rose, Whitemane. What you put down was the disease wearing their faces.
> whitemane: Say it again. Slowly. No one has ever said it to me.
> tirion: You didn't kill your family. The plague did. You set them free.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=icecrown
whitemane: Icecrown. Your champions are chosen, Fordring. Am I among them?
> tirion: You're here, aren't you?
> whitemane: That is not an answer.
> tirion: It's the only one I've got. I'll take anyone who walks up that Citadel with me. Scarlet included.
> whitemane: Then the Crusade will be at the top when it falls. See that it is remembered.
