# Prince Arthas and the High Inquisitor, who has burnt her own family and thinks the Culling was right.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Where are you from, Inquisitor? Before all this.
> whitemane: Hillsbrad. My family took the north road when the plague began. They did not reach the end of it.
> arthas: I'm sorry. I'm trying to stop it. That's why I'm out here.
> whitemane: I know how it is stopped, my prince. I had to stop my own family that way.
> arthas: ...Inquisitor, I...
> whitemane: Do not. I have never asked anyone for pity. I will not begin with you.

@ trigger=idle
whitemane: You are on the road to Stratholme, my prince. I know what you do there. The whole world knows. You purge it before the grain can turn them.
> arthas: I haven't decided anything. Uther would call it murder. Jaina would never forgive me.
> whitemane: Then they would be wrong. The Crusade has purged a hundred villages since Stratholme burned, and the Light was with us in every one.
> arthas: Is that meant to make me feel better?
> whitemane: It is meant to be true, my prince. I am not in the business of comfort.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Your commander. Mograine. I watched you bring him back from the dead.
> whitemane: The Light brought him back. I asked.
> arthas: Kel'Thuzad's plague raised the dead too. In Andorhal. I killed him for it.
> whitemane: My champion rises because the Light wills it. Kel'Thuzad's dead rose because he did. Do not confuse the two again.

@ trigger=idle
arthas: Do you ever smile, Inquisitor?
> whitemane: When the impure burn.
> arthas: That isn't funny.
> whitemane: It was not a joke. ...Your face, however, was amusing.

@ trigger=zone_enter where=scarlet_monastery
whitemane: The Cathedral. The last true house of the Light in Lordaeron.
> arthas: I've ridden past this monastery a hundred times. It was monks and bells.
> whitemane: Now it is the Crusade's. We hold it against the dead, and against the Forsaken who squat in your father's city.
> arthas: Squat? Inquisitor, my father's city is at the end of this road.
> whitemane: Then walk to the end of this road, my prince, and see what lives there now.

@ trigger=revived
arthas: Was I... was I dead just then?
> whitemane: Briefly. Do not make a habit of it. I already have one champion who does.
> arthas: Thank you, Inquisitor.
> whitemane: Thank the Light. And stay out of reach of their blades next time.
