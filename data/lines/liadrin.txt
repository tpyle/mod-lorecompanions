# Lady Liadrin - protection paladin, Blood Knight Matriarch of Silvermoon.
# Once a priestess of the Light, she lost her faith when Arthas burned
# Quel'Thalas, founded the Blood Knights and took her power from the naaru
# M'uru, captive of Kael'thas. When Kael'thas betrayed them and M'uru gave
# himself to rekindle the Sunwell, she found the Light again, freely given
# this time. Proud, fierce, disciplined, honest about what she was. She
# stands at the front; it is where she has decided she belongs.
