# Crusade, one of Tirion Fordring's champions at the Argent Tournament in
# Icecrown, where she tests challengers by calling up the memories of the
# foes they have faced. Gentle, perceptive and unafraid, she listens far
# more than she speaks, and when she speaks she tends to see straight
# through people. She believes in redemption, sometimes more than the
# people she believes it for. Her tournament line: "Take this time to
# consider your past deeds."
